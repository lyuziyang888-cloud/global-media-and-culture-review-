# Nation Branding and Othering

## Relationship

Nation branding can use Othering when it makes a country attractive by presenting its people, culture or landscape as different experiences for foreign audiences.

## Mechanism

```text
country selects marketable national images
-> foreign visitor becomes the main viewpoint
-> local culture becomes something to explore or consume
-> complex identities are simplified for tourism or investment
```

## Example

Qatar's *Surprise Yourself* videos centre international visitors experiencing deserts, resorts, markets and Arabic visual culture. The visitors act and discover, while local perspectives have less control over the story.

## Judgement

Othering can make a destination distinctive and easy to market, but it may organise national identity around foreign expectations. A strong answer should examine who acts, who is observed and whose perspective is missing.
