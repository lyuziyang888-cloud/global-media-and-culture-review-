# Media Imperialism and Cultural Globalisation

## Relationship In One Sentence

Media imperialism explains concentrated structures and historical continuity, while cultural globalisation explains active meaning-making, non-US capacity and contemporary change.

## Comparison

| Media imperialism | Cultural globalisation |
|---|---|
| macro political-economic structures | micro cultural practices and identifications |
| ownership, production and distribution | audiences, workers, meanings and identities |
| unequal and one-way flows | multidirectional and contra-flows |
| continuity with past power | changes in present media patterns |
| risk: structural determinism | risk: abstract celebration of diversity |

## Middle-Ground Mechanism

```text
unequal ownership and platform infrastructure
-> shape which cultural products can circulate
-> audiences and producers interpret, adapt and redirect them
-> cultural agency changes outcomes without erasing structural inequality
```

## Reusable Judgement

The paradigms ask different questions, so evidence can support both. A strong answer identifies whether the case primarily concerns ownership and control, or movement and meaning, then uses the other paradigm to test the limitation.
