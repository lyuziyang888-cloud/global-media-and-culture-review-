# Location and Soft Power

## Relationship

A location can create soft power by attracting foreign producers through its reputation, facilities, workers and professional relationships.

## Mechanism

```text
government incentives and local production services
-> positive experiences for foreign producers
-> stronger film-friendly reputation
-> investment, repeat production and international influence
```

## Example

Budapest attracts international productions through rebates, studios, skilled crews and workers who connect foreign producers with local institutions. It can appear as itself or stand in for other cities, giving it industrial value even when audiences do not recognise it on screen.

## Judgement

Media-service work can generate soft power without directly promoting a national image to audiences. However, dependence on mobile foreign productions and the frequent erasure of Budapest's identity limit Hungary's control.
