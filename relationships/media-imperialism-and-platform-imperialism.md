# Media Imperialism and Platform Imperialism

## Relationship In One Sentence

Platform imperialism renews media imperialism by shifting unequal power from content and broadcasting toward digital infrastructure, data, algorithms, advertising and cross-border platform markets.

## Continuities

- concentration of ownership and control
- US-centred economic and ideological power
- unequal flows of capital, technology and culture
- pressure on local industries and alternatives
- support from political and trade power

## Changes

- users provide data and labour as well as consuming content
- platforms mediate communication rather than only producing programs
- network effects strengthen dominant firms
- algorithms shape visibility
- corporate and state surveillance interests can intersect

## Reusable Evidence

Facebook earns value from global users, controls communication infrastructure, lobbies governments and benefits from US-backed free-flow policies.

## Exam Answer Skeleton

Platform imperialism is best understood as an update rather than a completely separate theory. It retains the concern with concentrated cross-border power but identifies new resources and mechanisms: data, network effects, algorithms and digital infrastructure.
