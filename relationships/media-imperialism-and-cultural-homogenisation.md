# Media Imperialism and Cultural Homogenisation

## Relationship In One Sentence

Media imperialism may encourage cultural homogenisation by spreading dominant values and styles through unequal flows, but audiences and local cultures can reinterpret or resist them.

## Mechanism

```text
one-way flow of dominant media
-> repeated exposure to foreign standards, lifestyles and values
-> dominant culture becomes normal or desirable
-> local difference may be marginalised
```

## Limitation

Circulation is not the same as cultural effect. Audiences interpret media through language, family, education, religion and existing identity. Non-US content and local industries can also remain popular.

## Reusable Evidence

- The lecture links US media to capitalist consumption, individualism and cultural homogenisation.
- Disney and Hollywood illustrate the claim that media promotes an American image of the good life.
- Successful non-US industries challenge a simple universal-homogenisation conclusion.

## Exam Answer Skeleton

Media imperialism identifies the structural conditions that make some cultural forms globally visible. These flows can pressure diversity, but homogenisation is a possible outcome rather than a guaranteed one because audiences and local institutions retain interpretive and productive agency.
