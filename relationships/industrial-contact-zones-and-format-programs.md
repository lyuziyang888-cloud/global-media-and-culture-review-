# Industrial Contact Zones and Format Programs

## Relationship

Format programs create industrial contact zones because global format owners and local producers must negotiate which elements remain standard and which are adapted.

## Mechanism

```text
licensed format and global production knowledge
-> contact with local producers, audiences and market conditions
-> negotiation over stable and adaptable elements
-> local version shaped by both sides
```

## Example

In *The Traitors*, the core competition and interpersonal conflict remain recognisable, while locations, participants, prize money and performance styles change across national versions.

## Judgement

The contact-zone concept shows that format adaptation is not equal cultural mixing, but local producers are also not passive recipients. Their influence depends on market knowledge, institutional power and the format owner's control.
