# Global Integration and Media Capacity

## Relationship

Global integration can help a media industry build capacity, but international visibility alone does not prove that lasting capacity has developed.

## Mechanism

```text
access to global finance, technology and markets
-> local firms gain work, knowledge and wider circulation
-> skills, infrastructure and intellectual property may develop
-> lasting capacity depends on who retains control and value
```

## Example

Indian animation outsourcing can provide employment and technical experience, while foreign companies may continue to own the intellectual property. Seoul Digital Media City represents stronger capacity-building because firms, workers and infrastructure are concentrated in a cluster that can support repeated production.

## Judgement

Global integration creates opportunity and dependency at the same time. A producer moves beyond a peripheral position when it can retain skills, infrastructure and intellectual property and continue producing without complete reliance on foreign companies.
