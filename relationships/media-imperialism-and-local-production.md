# Media Imperialism and Local Production

## Relationship In One Sentence

Dominant foreign media can weaken local production by controlling distribution, setting standards and supplying cheaper content, while local policy, investment and audience demand can create resistance.

## Mechanism

```text
concentrated foreign ownership and production
-> greater distribution reach and lower content costs
-> imported media crowds out local programming
-> less revenue, visibility and capacity for local producers
```

## Why The Relationship Is Not Automatic

- Governments can use regulation, subsidies and local-content obligations.
- Audiences may prefer local languages, genres and stories.
- National and regional companies can become powerful second-tier firms.
- Global platforms can sometimes distribute local productions internationally.

## Reusable Evidence

- The lecture identifies cheap foreign programming as a threat to local production.
- The Canada case shows regulation and public funding attempting to sustain local media.
- The tutorial asks whether local industries and government policy can counter US-based platforms.

## Exam Answer Skeleton

Media imperialism can diminish local production because structurally advantaged foreign firms control more capital, content and distribution. This can crowd out domestic programming and make foreign standards appear normal. However, the effect depends on policy, local capacity and audience demand, so foreign circulation should not be treated as automatic local disappearance.
