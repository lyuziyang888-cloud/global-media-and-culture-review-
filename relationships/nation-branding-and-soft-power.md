# Nation Branding and Soft Power

## Relationship

Nation branding is a deliberate form of soft power that uses attractive national images to influence how foreign audiences perceive a country.

## Mechanism

```text
campaign selects appealing stories and symbols
-> foreign audiences associate the country with desired qualities
-> improved reputation may support tourism, trade or investment
-> influence depends on credibility and audience response
```

## Example

Qatar's *Surprise Yourself* campaign connects the country with safety, luxury, culture and adventure. By contrast, Hungary's film-location soft power develops partly through production incentives and working relationships with foreign media companies rather than direct tourism advertising.

## Judgement

Nation branding gives a government greater control over its message, but its openly promotional style may appear selective. Fictional media and industrial relationships can seem less direct, although governments have less control over the meanings audiences receive.
