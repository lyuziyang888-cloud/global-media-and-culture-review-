# Unit 3 Question Bank: Cultural Globalisation

## Definition and Recall

1. What is cultural globalisation?
2. Why is it a paradigm rather than a single theory?
3. Name and define the five scapes.
4. What is asymmetrical interdependence?
5. What is cultural proximity?
6. What is a contra-flow?
7. What is a media capital?

## Explain Relationships

1. How does cultural globalisation respond to media imperialism?
2. How can multidirectional and one-way flows coexist?
3. What is the relationship between cultural proximity and audience preference?
4. How do technoscapes and mediascapes interact?
5. How does asymmetrical interdependence connect the two paradigms?
6. What is the relationship between media capitals and nation-states?

## Compare

1. Compare the macro approach of media imperialism with the micro approach of cultural globalisation.
2. Compare one-way flows and contra-flows.
3. Compare a nation-based account with a media-capitals account.
4. Compare cultural proximity and cultural homogenisation.
5. Compare the strengths and limitations of both paradigms.

## Apply to Cases

1. Explain *Jane the Virgin* through contra-flow and cultural adaptation.
2. How do Chinese microdramas complicate media imperialism?
3. How might Chinese microdramas still support a structural power analysis?
4. Apply cultural proximity to a national streaming Top 10 list.
5. Does a non-US title in a Top 10 chart prove multidirectional flow?

## Discuss and Evaluate

1. Does cultural globalisation underestimate corporate power?
2. Does the popularity of non-US media disprove media imperialism?
3. Is cultural proximity a reliable predictor of audience choice?
4. Do multiple media capitals create a more equal media world?
5. Which paradigm better explains contemporary streaming?
6. Why does Mirrlees favour a middle-ground approach?

## Answer Pattern

```text
direct judgement
-> define the concept or paradigm
-> apply precise evidence
-> explain the cultural mechanism
-> identify structural power
-> test a limitation
-> refine the judgement
```

Do not treat content origin, chart presence or production location as proof of equal power. Distinguish production, distribution, circulation, consumption and ownership.
