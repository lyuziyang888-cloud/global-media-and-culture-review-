# Week 12 Lecture: Nollywood and Informality

## Review Questions and Answers

### What are the characteristics of Nollywood?

- Nollywood produces many films quickly for Nigerian and regional audiences.
- It uses several languages and combines formal media businesses with informal production and distribution.

### What is the role of marketers in Nollywood? Why have marketers made it difficult for outsiders to break into Nollywood?

- Marketers finance productions and control important distribution networks.
- Their businesses rely on personal relationships and market knowledge that outsiders do not easily access.

### What does digital distribution look like in Nigeria? How is it different to Global North experiences of digital distribution?

- Films circulate through downloads, phones, kiosks and physical markets as well as streaming.
- This system responds to expensive data, unreliable internet and uneven access to electricity.

### Why is Netflix facing challenges in Nigeria, while iROKOtv is doing better?

- Netflix depends more heavily on internet access and subscriptions that many viewers cannot easily afford.
- iROKOtv has adapted more closely to local access conditions and viewing practices.

### Why is studying Nollywood significant for global media studies?

- Nollywood shows that a media industry can grow through local networks and informal distribution.
- It challenges the assumption that every successful industry must follow a Western platform model.
