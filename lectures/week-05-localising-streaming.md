# Week 5 Lecture: Localising Streaming

## Central Purpose

The lecture compares Netflix and Hotstar in India to explain localisation and industrial contact zones.

## Lecture Structure

1. Media companies have long adapted programming to different markets; Star TV and MTV are earlier examples.
2. Industrial contact zones are sites of interaction and negotiation under asymmetrical but context-dependent power relations.
3. India's film industry contains several regional cinemas and languages; Bollywood is only Hindi-language cinema centred in Mumbai.
4. Hotstar follows an indigenized-global strategy based on Bollywood, regional languages and cricket.
5. Netflix initially follows a cosmopolitan-local strategy aimed at urban, liberal and globally oriented audiences.
6. Netflix becomes "Bollywoodized" by working with established Indian stars, directors and production entities.
7. India's scale, cultural diversity, government and local competitors limit Netflix's assumed dominance.

## Key Concepts and Definitions

- **Localisation:** adapting media content, services or business strategies to a particular market.
- **Industrial contact zone:** a site where global and local media actors interact under unequal but changing power relations.
- **Cosmopolitan-local:** targeting audiences who value globally oriented forms of local culture.
- **Indigenized-global:** combining global platform practices with local languages, genres and popular institutions.
- **Bollywoodization:** Netflix's increasing reliance on established Hindi-film talent, production practices and cultural forms.

## Main Arguments

- Localisation is an industrial negotiation, not a one-way transfer from global companies to passive local markets.
- Netflix has substantial capital and distribution power, but it must adapt to India's languages, industries and competitors.
- Hotstar globalises a locally grounded service, while Netflix localises a global service.
- The Indian case complicates a simple platform-imperialism explanation.

## Cases and Evidence

- **Hotstar:** uses Bollywood, regional-language content and cricket to build broad local appeal.
- **Netflix India:** initially targeted cosmopolitan viewers before working more closely with Bollywood institutions.
- **India's regional cinemas:** show why Bollywood cannot represent the entire national market.

## Assessment and Exam Signals

- What value does the industrial-contact-zone concept have?
- Compare cosmopolitan-local and indigenized-global strategies.
- What is the Bollywoodization of Netflix?
- How do Netflix and Hotstar challenge platform-imperialism theory?
