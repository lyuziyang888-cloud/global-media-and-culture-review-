# Week 5 Lecture: Localising Streaming

## Focus

The lecture compares Netflix and Hotstar in India to explain localisation and industrial contact zones.

## Key Sequence

1. Media companies have long adapted programming to different markets; Star TV and MTV are earlier examples.
2. Industrial contact zones are sites of interaction and negotiation under asymmetrical but context-dependent power relations.
3. India's film industry contains several regional cinemas and languages; Bollywood is only Hindi-language cinema centred in Mumbai.
4. Hotstar follows an indigenized-global strategy based on Bollywood, regional languages and cricket.
5. Netflix initially follows a cosmopolitan-local strategy aimed at urban, liberal and globally oriented audiences.
6. Netflix becomes "Bollywoodized" by working with established Indian stars, directors and production entities.
7. India's scale, cultural diversity, government and local competitors limit Netflix's assumed dominance.

## Lecture Questions

- What value does the industrial-contact-zone concept have?
- Compare cosmopolitan-local and indigenized-global strategies.
- What is the Bollywoodization of Netflix?
- How do Netflix and Hotstar challenge platform-imperialism theory?
