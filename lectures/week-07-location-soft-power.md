# Week 7 Lecture: Location as Soft Power

## Review Questions and Answers

### What is soft power? How can a place be "made"?

 Soft power is the ability to influence others through attraction.
 
 place making is the process through which locations acquires a particular meaning and identity.

### How does Budapest's position as an unfixed place compared to other media services industries in Canada or Australia?

Budapest often stands in for other cities, while Canada and Australia are more likely to promote their locations as part of their own national screen industries.

### What actions does the Hungarian government take to promote the country to foreign producers? What do the local "fixers" do that is different?

- The government attracts productions through tax incentives and official promotion.
- Fixers find locations, arrange permits and help foreign crews work with local people.

### Why are spy thrillers the best genre for generating soft power for Hungary? Also, how does this genre challenge existing understandings of soft power?

- Spy thrillers need flexible locations, so Budapest can represent several cities.
- Hungary can build influence through its relationships with foreign producers, even if audiences do not recognise Budapest on screen
