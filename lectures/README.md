## How the Exam Review Video Says to Use Lectures

1. Start with the review questions at the end of each lecture.
2. Answer each question without notes. Return to the lecture only when an answer is incomplete.
3. Learn the concept and how the lecture applies it. Names, dates and minor production details have lower priority.
4. For a short answer, answer the specific question directly and keep the response brief.
5. For a long answer, separate the question into its parts and answer them in order. Do not force every answer into one fixed template.
6. For the unseen news question, choose a relevant course concept and connect it to specific evidence from the excerpt.

Lecture maps preserve the teacher's definitions, argument sequence, questions and slide anchors. The exam-focus notes are revision directions only. They do not replace or rewrite the lecture questions.
