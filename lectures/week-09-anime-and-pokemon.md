# Week 9 Lecture: Anime and Pokémon

## Review Questions and Answers

### What is being culturally odorless and how has Japanese utilized this strategy in the audiovisual industry?

- Cultural odorlessness reduces obvious links between a media product and its country of origin.
- Japanese audiovisual products such as anime use visually ambiguous characters and settings that can travel without audiences immediately thinking of Japan.

### What is Japanese about Pokémon (and anime in general)?

- Pokémon comes from Japanese companies and production practices, but its Japanese identity is not always visible in the finished product.
- Its cultural odorlessness is therefore part of how Japanese media reaches global audiences.

### What is the Pokémon supersystem? What is the role of American distribution networks in Pokémon's globalisation strategy?

- The supersystem connects Pokémon across games, television, films, cards and merchandise.
- Nintendo of America adapted it for American audiences, while Warner Brothers helped distribute that version internationally.

### How does Iwabuchi (author) propose to move beyond the binary understanding of Asia/West and cute/cool?

- Iwabuchi rejects the idea that Asian audiences naturally prefer cute media while Western audiences prefer cool media.
- He focuses instead on how different audiences consume and interpret Pokémon.
