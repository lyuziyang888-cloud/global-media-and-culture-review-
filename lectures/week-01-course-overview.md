# Week 1 Lecture: Course Overview

## Review Questions and Answers

### Is *KPop Demon Hunters* an American film?

- It cannot be classified through one feature alone.
- Its ownership and distribution connect it to the United States, while its story, genre and cultural references connect it to Korea.

### What should we consider when deciding whether media is local, national or global?

- Consider who created and owns it, where it was produced, what culture it represents and how it reaches audiences.
- These factors may connect one media text to several places.

### Why do we study global media critically?

- Media crosses borders, but ownership, cultural identity and distribution remain unequal.
- Critical study asks who controls media, whose culture appears and how local industries respond.
