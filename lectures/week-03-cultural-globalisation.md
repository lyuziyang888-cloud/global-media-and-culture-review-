# Week 3 Lecture: Cultural Globalisation

## Review Questions and Answers

### What are the definition, characteristics, limitations, and value of cultural (media) imperialism and cultural globalisation paradigms?

- Media imperialism focuses on unequal ownership and mainly one-way flows. It explains structural power but can overlook audiences and local adaptation.
- Cultural globalisation focuses on multidirectional flows and cultural interaction. It explains change and audience choice but can underestimate continuing inequality.

### Can you identify and define theories/concepts associated with media imperialism and cultural globalisation?

- Media imperialism includes dependency theory, world-systems theory and platform imperialism.
- Cultural globalisation includes cultural proximity, contra-flows, asymmetrical interdependence, the five-scapes model and media capitals.

### Can you discuss specific examples that are best explained by media imperialism and cultural globalisation?

- Facebook is explained well by media imperialism because platform ownership and data power remain concentrated.
- *Jane the Virgin* is explained well by cultural globalisation because a Venezuelan story travelled and was adapted in several countries.

### What was the author (Mirrlees) arguing in Chapter 1? Why does he mention cultural materialism?

- Mirrlees argues that neither paradigm explains global media completely.
- Cultural materialism connects cultural meaning with the economic and political conditions in which media is produced and circulated.
