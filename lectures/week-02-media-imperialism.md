# Week 2 Lecture: Media Imperialism

## Review Questions and Answers

### What is media imperialism?

- Media imperialism explains how powerful countries or companies dominate other media systems.
- This power can come from ownership, infrastructure and mainly one-way media flows.

### What theories and concepts are associated with media imperialism?

- Dependency theory explains continuing dependence on powerful countries.
- World-systems theory divides the global system into unequal positions.
- Platform imperialism applies the argument to digital platforms and their control of technology and data.

### What are the value and limitations of media imperialism?

- It is useful for explaining unequal ownership and corporate power.
- It can overlook active audiences, local adaptation and successful media industries outside the West.

### How does platform imperialism update media imperialism?

- Powerful platforms can extend control into other countries through infrastructure and data.
- Facebook illustrates how a US platform can gain influence while operating across many national markets.
