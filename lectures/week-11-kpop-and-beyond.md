# Week 11 Lecture: The Possibilities of K-pop

## Review Questions and Answers

### What is a transcultural approach to K-pop fandom?

- It explains fandom through shared interests across cultural and national boundaries.
- Nationality alone does not explain how fans connect with K-pop.

### What is cultural hybridity? What are the ways that K-pop is a culturally hybrid form, in terms of industrial and fan practices?

- Cultural hybridity is the mixing and reworking of cultural forms.
- K-pop companies use international creative workers and musical influences, while fans adapt performances through practices such as cover dance.

### How does K-pop overcome the cultural discount effect?

- K-pop combines recognisable global styles with Korean performance and industry practices.
- Visual performance and online circulation also make it accessible beyond language differences.

### What are the three types of experiences of cultural hybridity (in Yoon article)? Can you identify, define, and apply each type to examples?

- **Participatory globalisation:** fans actively adapt and circulate K-pop, such as producing cover dances.
- **Cosmopolitan globalisation:** fans connect K-pop with different places and identities.
- **Cute globalisation:** fans view idols as softer and more approachable than dominant Western pop images.
