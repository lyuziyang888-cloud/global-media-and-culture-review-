# Hollywood, the Pentagon and Militainment

## What It Demonstrates

- media and political power
- ideology and foreign policy
- cultural influence
- institutional cooperation
- limits of treating entertainment as politically neutral

## Case Summary

The lecture uses cooperation between Hollywood and the US Pentagon as an example of "militainment." Entertainment can circulate images of military power and ideas that support US capitalism or foreign policy while appearing primarily commercial or recreational.

## Analytical Chain

```text
institutional cooperation
-> access, support or influence over representation
-> globally distributed entertainment
-> US military power becomes familiar or legitimate
```

## Best Exam Use

Use the case to connect media imperialism with ideological, political and economic imperialism. It is stronger when the answer explains institutional power rather than merely stating that a film is American.

## Limitation

Exposure does not guarantee agreement. Audiences can interpret, reject or repurpose the message, and each production requires specific evidence before claiming direct Pentagon influence.
