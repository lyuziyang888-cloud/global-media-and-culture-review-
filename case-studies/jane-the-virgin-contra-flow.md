# Jane the Virgin: Contra-Flow and Adaptation

## Case Summary

The Venezuelan telenovela *Juana la virgen* (2002) was remade in Poland, the US, Turkey, Greece, Israel, South Korea, Mexico and India. The lecture compares the US CW and Indian JioHotstar versions.

## Concepts Supported

- contra-flow
- multidirectional media flow
- cultural globalisation
- local adaptation
- cultural proximity
- media formats

## Analytical Use

The format moved from a location outside the traditional US core into several established and emerging markets. Each remake could retain the basic format while adapting language, humour, values and social expectations for local audiences.

## Limitation

Format movement alone does not reveal ownership, licensing revenue, platform control or the relative global reach of each adaptation.

## Possible Question

How does *Jane the Virgin* challenge a one-way model of global media flow?
