# Unit 1 Exam Examples

| Concept | Example | Direct exam use |
|---|---|---|
| local content | *Heated Rivalry* uses Canadian creators, labour, locations and imagery | Locality depends on production and representation, not only distribution territory. |
| global/local relationship | HBO distributed *Heated Rivalry* in the US | A locally identifiable production can gain global reach without becoming culturally placeless. |
| Indigenous representation | *North of North* is largely Indigenous-created, acted and produced | Representation is shaped by who participates in authorship and production. |
| platform power | Netflix distributes *North of North* internationally | Platforms provide reach but retain important distribution power. |
| media regulation | proposed 15% Canadian streaming levy | Governments use policy to make global platforms support domestic culture. |
| contested media nationality | *KPop Demon Hunters* can be assessed through cast, crew, story, genre, ownership and location | A media text cannot always be assigned one simple national identity. |

## Best Three to Memorise

1. *Heated Rivalry*: local content plus global distribution.
2. *North of North*: Indigenous authorship plus Netflix reach.
3. Canadian streaming levy: regulation versus platform power.
