# UNESCO, the MacBride Report and NWICO

## What It Demonstrates

- unequal global information flows
- concentrated communication infrastructure
- conflict between balanced flows and free-market access
- links between communication and postcolonial power

## Case Summary

UNESCO debates highlighted the dominance of Western news agencies and Western control over radio spectrum and satellite allocation. The New World Information and Communication Order called for more balanced communication between developed and developing countries.

Western countries instead supported a free flow of information with fewer barriers. From a media-imperialism perspective, formally open flows can preserve inequality when countries begin with very different ownership, capital and infrastructure.

## Best Exam Use

Use the case to show that media imperialism concerns the structure and direction of global communication, not only the cultural content of Hollywood products.

## Limitation

The historical evidence explains the paradigm's origins. Current platform and media markets require updated evidence rather than assuming that the same institutions and statistics remain unchanged.
