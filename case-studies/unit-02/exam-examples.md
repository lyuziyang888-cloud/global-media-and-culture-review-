# Unit 2 Exam Examples

| Concept | Example | Direct exam use |
|---|---|---|
| one-way flow | historically Western news agencies supplied much of the world's news | Media imperialism identifies unequal control over international information flows. |
| cultural homogenisation | cheap foreign programs can displace local production and circulate US commercial values | Unequal flows may reduce production diversity and normalise dominant values. |
| media-state power | Hollywood-Pentagon cooperation, often called militainment | Entertainment can support state ideology and foreign-policy interests. |
| platform capitalism | Facebook monetises user attention and data through targeted advertising | Participation creates private commercial value for the platform owner. |
| platform imperialism | Facebook was the leading social network in 152 of 167 analysed countries in 2018 | Global scale and network effects concentrate technological and economic power. |
| asymmetrical capital flow | users are global while Facebook ownership and accumulation are US-centred | Interdependence exists, but its benefits and control are unequal. |

## Best Three to Memorise

1. Facebook data commodification: platform capitalism.
2. Facebook's 152-of-167-country dominance: platform imperialism.
3. Hollywood-Pentagon cooperation: media, ideology and state power.
