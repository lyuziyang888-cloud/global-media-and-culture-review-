# Chinese Microdramas: Global Popularity

## Case Summary

The tutorial uses the overseas popularity and revenue growth of Chinese microdramas to test media imperialism, cultural globalisation and the role of streaming services.

## Concepts Supported

- contra-flow
- non-US production capacity
- cultural globalisation
- platform power
- asymmetrical interdependence
- local-industry opportunity

## Cultural-Globalisation Interpretation

Chinese-origin formats and production practices can reach audiences outside China, complicating a one-way US-to-rest model and demonstrating non-US creative capacity.

## Media-Imperialism Interpretation

Distribution, app-store access, recommendation, data and monetisation may still be concentrated. A new successful content flow does not establish equal control or revenue.

## Limitation

The tutorial presents the case as a discussion prompt. Verify current viewing, download and revenue figures against the original sources before using statistics.

## Possible Question

Does Chinese microdrama popularity weaken media imperialism or reveal a new form of platform dependency?
