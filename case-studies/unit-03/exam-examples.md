# Unit 3 Exam Examples

| Concept | Example | Direct exam use |
|---|---|---|
| competing paradigms | Lucas reads US entertainment exports as Americanisation; Legrain reads them as exchange and hybridity | The same trend can generate different explanations depending on the analytical lens. |
| contra-flow | Venezuela's *Juana la Virgen* was adapted in the US, South Korea, India and other markets | Media flows can move from outside the traditional US core in several directions. |
| cultural proximity | audiences often prefer content sharing language, humour, religion or lifestyle | Audience choice is shaped by cultural closeness, not only industrial supply. |
| media capitals | centres with concentrated talent, reputation and resources attract production | Global flows are organised through dynamic industry centres rather than nations alone. |
| cultural globalisation with inequality | Chinese microdramas circulate globally through streaming platforms | Non-US creativity can expand while platforms still exercise distribution power. |
| paradigm synthesis | analyse local adaptation and audience meaning alongside ownership and revenue | Cultural agency does not automatically eliminate structural inequality. |

## Best Three to Memorise

1. Lucas versus Legrain: the two paradigms.
2. *Juana la Virgen/Jane the Virgin*: contra-flow and adaptation.
3. Chinese microdramas: non-US circulation plus platform-power limitation.
