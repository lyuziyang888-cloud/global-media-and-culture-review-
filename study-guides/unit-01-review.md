# Unit 1 Review: What Is Global Media?

## Must Know

- **Global media:** media produced, distributed or consumed across borders.
- **Local content:** content connected to a place through creators, stories, language, funding or production.
- **Key point:** global and local are not opposites. One media text can be local, national and global.

```text
ownership + creators + story + location + funding + distribution
-> media identity
```

## Best Examples

- ***Heated Rivalry*:** Canadian creators and locations, but global distribution.
- ***North of North*:** Indigenous-led Canadian production distributed through Netflix.
- **CanCon regulation:** government policy supports Canadian production and identity.

[More evidence](../exam-evidence/unit-01.md)

## Discussion Questions

### What makes media Canadian?

Look at creators, cast, crew, funding, ownership, locations and cultural symbols. No single factor decides it.

### What is local content?

Content with meaningful local creative or industrial participation. Filming in a country is not enough by itself.

### Is *Heated Rivalry* Western media?

Yes, but “Canadian-rooted and globally distributed” is more precise than simply calling it Western.

### Can geography explain global and local media?

No. A program can be filmed in one country, owned in another and watched worldwide.

## Exam Answer Pattern

```text
define the label
-> use several criteria
-> apply one example
-> identify global and local elements
-> give a clear judgement
```

**Useful judgement:** Media identity is multidimensional. Global distribution does not erase local culture, but platform ownership can create unequal power.
