# Unit 7 Review: Location as Soft Power

## Must Know

- **Soft power:** influencing others through attraction rather than coercion or payment.
- **Place-making:** governments, industries and media construct the meanings attached to a location.
- **Media-service industry:** local companies and workers provide locations, facilities and labour for outside productions.
- **Fixers:** intermediaries connecting government, foreign producers and local stakeholders.

## Best Examples

- **Budapest:** foreign production investment grew nearly 500% from 2018 to 2024, reaching almost US$1 billion per year. Its 30% rebate, locations and crews help market it as film-friendly.
- **Hungarian film diplomacy:** official agencies promote incentives and facilities; informal fixers build trust and translate between foreign producers, government and local workers.
- ***The Day of the Jackal* (2024):** most production took place in Hungary, and Budapest represented Paris, Munich and Istanbul as well as itself.
- ***Boy Swallows Universe*:** constructs a historically and socially specific Brisbane rather than an interchangeable location.

## Key Questions

### How can location create soft power?

A place can attract producers, investment and audiences by presenting appealing locations, skills and cultural meanings. Day-to-day production relationships can therefore contribute to soft power.

### What makes Budapest an unfixed place?

Budapest can represent a modern European destination, an Eastern European geopolitical setting or another city entirely. Its meaning changes with genre and production purpose.

### Are media-service industries simply exploited by Hollywood?

Not simply. Hungary gains investment, expertise and a strong service reputation. However, foreign companies can relocate, domestic production remains limited, and Budapest is often erased on screen. Its success therefore combines local capacity with external dependence.

## Tutorial Exam Practice

**Question:** How is place understood similarly in media capitals and spy thrillers filmed in Hungary?

**Short answer:** Both show that place is produced through changing history, industry and representation. Media-capital status can rise or fall, while Budapest changes meaning when it represents itself or another city.

## Exam Answer Pattern

```text
define soft power or place-making
-> identify who constructs the place
-> explain the intended audience and purpose
-> apply Budapest or Brisbane
-> identify what the representation includes and excludes
-> judge the benefit and limitation
```

**Useful judgement:** A flexible location can gain industrial value by being visible as a production centre while remaining visually invisible on screen.
