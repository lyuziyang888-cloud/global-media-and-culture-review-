# Units 1-3 Exam Prep

This is the main revision page for Units 1-3. Use the individual unit guides only when a topic below needs more detail.

## The Three-Unit Story

```text
Unit 1 asks what makes media global, local or national
-> Unit 2 explains unequal ownership, infrastructure and one-way flows
-> Unit 3 explains audiences, multidirectional flows and non-US media success
-> strong evaluation combines cultural movement with structural inequality
```

## Priority 2: Relationships to Explain

1. **Global and local:** global circulation can expand local visibility, but ownership and platform dependence shape whose terms prevail.
2. **Globalisation and identity:** cross-border media can hybridise identity while local institutions and representation preserve difference.
3. **Media imperialism and local production:** concentrated ownership and imports can constrain local capacity, while policy and audience demand can create resistance.
4. **Media and platform imperialism:** platforms renew imperial power through data, infrastructure, algorithms and market access.
5. **Media imperialism and cultural globalisation:** the first explains structural inequality; the second explains cultural agency, adaptation and multidirectional flow.
6. **One-way flow and contra-flow:** both may operate simultaneously; contra-flow does not prove equal ownership or revenue.
7. **Cultural proximity and audiences:** familiarity influences preference but does not determine it.
8. **Media capitals and nation-states:** production clusters can cross national boundaries while still depending on policy, finance and infrastructure.

## High-Value Comparisons

| Comparison | Main distinction | Strong evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Global vs local | scale and connection vs cultural or institutional specificity | they interact rather than form a strict binary |
| Media imperialism vs cultural globalisation | structural power vs cultural agency and complexity | use both according to the question and evidence |
| One-way flow vs contra-flow | dominant direction vs counter-direction | circulation alone does not reveal ownership or profit |
| Classical vs platform imperialism | states and media firms vs data-driven platforms | platforms update rather than completely replace earlier inequality |
| Cultural homogenisation vs cultural proximity | convergence toward dominant culture vs preference for familiarity | both can occur in different markets and moments |

## Universal Answer Structure

```text
1. Define the key concept accurately.
2. Give a direct answer or judgement.
3. Explain the mechanism or relationship.
4. Apply one precise case fact.
5. Test the claim with a limitation or competing paradigm.
6. Conclude by answering the exact question.
```

## Seven-Day Revision Route

1. **Day 1:** memorise Unit 1 definitions and classify one media text.
2. **Day 2:** memorise Unit 2 definitions and explain its argument chain.
3. **Day 3:** memorise Unit 3 definitions and explain its argument chain.
4. **Day 4:** practise the five high-value comparisons aloud.
5. **Day 5:** build four case-study evidence cards from memory.
6. **Day 6:** answer two timed questions from the consolidated question bank.
7. **Day 7:** complete one mock set and repair only the gaps it exposes.
