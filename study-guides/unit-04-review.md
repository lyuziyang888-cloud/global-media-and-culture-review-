# Unit 4 Review: Format Programs

## Must Know

- **Canned program:** the finished program is exported without being remade.
- **Format program:** a licensed production package is remade locally.
- **Non-scripted format:** keeps rules and structure but changes participants and cultural details.
- **Scripted format:** adapts story, characters and dialogue for another market.

```text
global format rules + local adaptation = format program
```

## Best Example: *The Traitors*

**Stays the same:** traitors, faithful contestants, missions, banishment and elimination.

**Can change:** location, host, contestants, prize money, tone and local celebrity culture.

## Tutorial Discussion Questions

### Are national versions unique or the same?

Both. The licensed structure stays recognisable, while local producers change cultural and production details.

### Does format trade challenge West-versus-Rest thinking?

Yes. Formats can move in several directions. However, ownership and licensing revenue can remain unequal.

### Why are formats useful?

They reduce production risk because the concept has already succeeded elsewhere, while still allowing localisation.

### Which theory explains formats?

Cultural globalisation explains adaptation and local agency. Media imperialism explains ownership and licensing power.

## Exam Answer Pattern

```text
define format program
-> identify fixed elements
-> identify local changes
-> explain global/local interaction
-> evaluate ownership and power
```

**Useful judgement:** Formats show that standardisation and localisation happen together. They support cultural exchange, but do not guarantee equal industrial power.
