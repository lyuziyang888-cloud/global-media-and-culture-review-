# Dependency Theory

## Definition

Dependency theory argues that capitalist development can reproduce unequal relationships in which developing or postcolonial countries remain economically and structurally dependent on powerful Anglo-European countries.

## Keywords

- dependency
- postcolonial inequality
- capitalism
- centre and periphery
- unequal development
- structural power

## Core Understanding

- Formal independence does not guarantee equal economic power.
- Wealth and decision-making remain concentrated in developed centres.
- Peripheral countries can supply markets, labour or resources without gaining equal control.
- Media systems can reproduce the same dependency through ownership, technology and content.
- Dependency theory supplies part of the historical explanation for media imperialism: unequal media flows are connected to wider political and economic relationships, not simply audience preferences.
