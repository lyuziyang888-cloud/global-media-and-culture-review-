# Pokémon Localisation

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Pokémon localisation adapted the Japanese franchise for American audiences.

## Mechanism And Evidence

Nintendo of America changed names, scenes and music. Warner Brothers helped distribute the adapted version.

## Exam Use

> What role did American distribution networks play in Pokémon's globalisation?

**Answer:** They adapted Pokémon for American audiences and then helped circulate it internationally.
