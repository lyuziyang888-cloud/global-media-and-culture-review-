# Pokémon Localisation

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Pokémon localisation adapted the Japanese franchise for American audiences before distributing that version more widely.

## Mechanism And Evidence

Nintendo of America and Warner Brothers changed character names, edited scenes and episodes, replaced music and made moral conflict clearer for American children and parents.

## Exam Use

> What role did American distribution networks play in Pokémon's globalisation?

**Answer:** Nintendo of America adapted Pokémon for American audiences, while Warner Brothers supported its distribution. The Americanised version was then circulated more widely.
