# Cultural Odor and Odorlessness

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

**Cultural odor** means a product clearly shows its country of origin. **Cultural odorlessness** means these national signs are reduced.

## Mechanism And Evidence

Pokémon has few obvious Japanese signs, so international audiences can enjoy it without recognising it as Japanese.

## Exam Use

> What is being culturally odorless, and how has Japan used this strategy in the audiovisual industry?

**Answer:** Cultural odorlessness means reducing signs of a product's national origin. Pokémon uses few obvious Japanese signs, which helps it reach global audiences.
