# Cultural Odor and Odorlessness

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

**Cultural odor** appears when audiences associate a product with its country of origin and way of life. **Cultural odorlessness** weakens that association, so audiences do not immediately think of the country of origin.

## Mechanism And Evidence

Pokémon softens clear racial and national characteristics. Its characters and fictional world do not require audiences to recognise Japan.

## Exam Use

> What is being culturally odorless, and how has Japan used this strategy in the audiovisual industry?

**Answer:** Cultural odorlessness reduces obvious links to a product's country of origin. Pokémon uses visually ambiguous characters and settings, which helps it travel without requiring audiences to recognise Japan.
