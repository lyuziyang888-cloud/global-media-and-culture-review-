# Platform Imperialism

**Unit:** Unit 2: Media Imperialism
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Definition

Platform imperialism is when powerful global platforms extend control over media systems in other countries.

## How It Works

Platform imperialism extends imperialism analysis from exported media texts to digital infrastructure. A dominant platform enters many national markets, gathers data from their users, connects local businesses to its advertising system and applies centrally designed technical standards. Capital and information then move back toward the corporation's home base, while local actors become dependent on access to its network.

## Key Terms

- asymmetry
- US dominance
- technological exchange
- capital flows
- global expansion
- symbolic power
- geopolitical power

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Facebook temporarily blocked Australian news content during conflict over the News Media Bargaining Code.
- The action showed that a foreign platform could use control of audience access to pressure a national news system.

## How The Evidence Applies

Facebook's position as the leading social network in 152 of 167 analysed countries indicates exceptional international reach. Reach becomes imperial power when combined with control: Facebook owns the software, ranking systems and commercial data generated through those markets. Its Australian news block demonstrated leverage in practice because it could interrupt access to news while contesting national regulation.

## Critical Qualification

The case does not establish complete US control. China restricts major US platforms, regional services can compete, and states can impose regulation. Platform imperialism is therefore an argument about asymmetrical bargaining power, not the disappearance of national institutions or local agency.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** How does platform imperialism extend media imperialism?


**Do not overclaim:** Corporate reach does not prove identical audience responses. Local industries, regulation and user practices can limit platform power.

## Derived Short-Answer Practice

> How does platform imperialism differ from exporting American programs?

**Answer:** The platform controls software, data, ranking and market access across countries. Facebook can extract value from locally produced communication without producing every item of content itself.
