# One-Way Media Flow

**Unit:** Unit 2: Media Imperialism
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Memorise This

One-way media flow describes an unequal pattern in which information and entertainment move mainly from powerful Western or US centres to the rest of the world rather than through balanced reciprocal exchange.

## Detailed Definition

One-way media flow describes an unequal pattern in which information and entertainment move mainly from powerful Western or US centres to the rest of the world rather than through balanced reciprocal exchange.

## Key Terms

- imbalance
- centre to periphery
- Western news agencies
- Hollywood
- infrastructure
- reciprocity

## Key Points

- Powerful countries control more news agencies, spectrum, satellites and production capacity.
- Their media reaches global audiences more easily than media from less powerful regions.
- Cheap imported content can crowd out local programming.
- Flow includes technology, standards and ideology as well as programs.
- One-way flow is a central mechanism in classic media imperialism. It turns the abstract claim of domination into a question about who sends, who receives and who controls the routes.

## PPT Case

**Case:** Hollywood and major Western news agencies

**Why it fits:** Their production resources and distribution reach historically enabled content to travel outward more easily than media from peripheral regions.
