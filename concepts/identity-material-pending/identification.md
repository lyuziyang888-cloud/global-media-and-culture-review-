# Identification

**Unit:** Identity Material: Unit Pending Confirmation

## Definition

Identification is the process of recognising, choosing or feeling connection with a person, group, role or identity position.

## Keywords

- active process
- recognition
- belonging
- similarity
- identity position
- choice and constraint

## Core Understanding

- Identity is not identical to personality; it involves active engagement.
- People identify with groups by recognising shared characteristics or experiences.
- Identification also marks difference from people outside the group.
- Some identities offer more choice than others because structures limit available positions.
- Identification shows that identity is something people do, not merely something they possess. It connects personal recognition with social categories and group belonging.

## Possible Questions

- What is identification?
- How does identification differ from personality?
- Explain how identification produces belonging and difference.
