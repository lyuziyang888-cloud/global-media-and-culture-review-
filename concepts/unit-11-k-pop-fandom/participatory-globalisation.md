# Participatory Globalisation

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Participatory globalisation means audiences help create and circulate global media.

## Course Evidence And Application

RISIN learned and publicly performed BLACKPINK's choreography in Paris. The commenter @pbjsangweech praised the “subtle changes to the original choreo.” The dancers transform a commercial text into their own performance, and the viewer evaluates the changes.

## Exam Use

## Derived Short-Answer Practice

> What does the comment about subtle changes reveal about participation?

**Answer:** The viewer notices and values fan adaptation, not only accurate copying. The dancers act on the original choreography and the audience evaluates their creative choices.

**Long:** Explain the fan action, adaptation and new circulation route, then ask who benefits.

**Limit:** Participation can be commodified; YouTube and agencies gain attention from fan labour (Yoon, 2018, p. 380).

**Source:** Yoon (2018), p. 380.
