# Participatory Globalisation

**Unit:** Unit 11 K Pop Fandom
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Definition

Fans imagine and experience globalisation through active participation in media culture rather than through consumption alone.

## What This Concept Explains

Fans imagine and experience globalisation through active participation in media culture rather than through consumption alone.

## Key Terms

participation; re-appropriation; cover dance; reaction video; fan labour

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Yoon identifies reaction videos, parodies, fan forums and grassroots cover-dance groups as ways fans re-appropriate K-pop texts.
- Cover dancers reproduce recognisable choreography while changing performers, locations and presentation.

## How the Evidence Shows the Concept

Cover dances, reaction videos, fan chants and online communities let fans interact with and adapt K-pop texts. Participation creates agency, although platforms and entertainment companies may still gain value from unpaid fan activity.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** How do K-pop fans participate in globalisation?

**Answer order:** definition -> relevant evidence above -> explain the connection -> limitation -> judgement.

**Do not overclaim:** Fan agency is real but not complete freedom: participation can create value for entertainment companies, advertisers and platforms, and one case cannot represent every fan.

**Course basis:** Week 11 tutorial; Yoon, p. 380; Jin, p. 133

## Possible Short Answer

> What is Participatory Globalisation? Give one course example.

**Answer:** Fans imagine and experience globalisation through active participation in media culture rather than through consumption alone. A course example is K-pop cover dance. Fans learn, perform, modify and circulate choreography instead of remaining consumers only.
