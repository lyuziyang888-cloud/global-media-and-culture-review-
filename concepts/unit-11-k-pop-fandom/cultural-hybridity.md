# Cultural Hybridity

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Cultural hybridity is the mixing and reworking of different cultural forms.

## Course Evidence And Application

Yoon identifies reaction videos, parodies, forums and cover dances as fan re-appropriation (p. 380). RISIN's “How You Like That” cover keeps BLACKPINK's choreography recognisable but changes the performers, Paris location and visual presentation. It is neither unrelated creation nor exact copy.

## Exam Use

## Official PPT Exam Practice

The Week 11 tutorial, slide 4, asks:

> Apply cultural hybridity to KATSEYE's industrial strategy. Give specific examples.

Use the HYBE-Geffen partnership, multinational membership, K-pop training system and multilingual versions of “Touch.” Explain fusion and disruption of fixed categories, then note that commercially organised hybridity does not guarantee success.

## Derived Short-Answer Practice

> Is RISIN's cover simply a copy of BLACKPINK?

**Answer:** Not completely. It keeps the choreography recognisable but changes the performers, gender presentation, Paris location and visual style. Hybridity appears in the combination of continuity and alteration.

**Long:** Name what is retained, what changes and who controls the adaptation.

**Limit:** Adaptation still circulates corporate intellectual property.

**Source:** Yoon (2018), pp. 374-380.
