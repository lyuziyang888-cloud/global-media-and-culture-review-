# Soft Power

**Unit:** Unit 7: Location as Soft Power
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Definition

Soft power is the ability to influence others through attraction.

## How It Operates In Media Services

Havens and Imre extend soft power from attracting foreign publics to attracting foreign media professionals. Hungary constructs Budapest as affordable, skilled, flexible and production-friendly so that producers voluntarily choose it. Tax rebates are a material incentive, but the city's reputation, professional relationships and visual adaptability supply the attractive image that makes the policy effective.

## Key Terms

- attraction
- influence
- culture
- diplomacy
- image

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Foreign production investment in Hungary grew nearly 500% from 2018 to 2024 and reached almost US$1 billion per year.
- A 30% rebate, studios, varied locations and skilled crews support Budapest’s film-friendly reputation.
- Budapest frequently represents other European cities, so the location can gain industrial value while disappearing from the finished story.

## How The Case Demonstrates It

Hungary offers a 30% rebate, studios, experienced crews and locations able to represent Paris, Munich, Istanbul or Budapest. Almost US$1 billion in annual film spending and the concentration of *The Day of the Jackal* production in Hungary show that the image produces industrial decisions, not only favourable opinion. The limit is ownership: attracting mobile foreign projects does not guarantee Hungarian control of stories, rights or global distribution.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** How can filming locations generate soft power?

**How to answer:** Identify the target of attraction, the image and relationships used, the resulting decision and the control that remains external.

**Do not overclaim:** Media-service production can create investment and expertise, but foreign projects can relocate and may leave local producers dependent or visually erase the location.

**Course basis:** Week 7 lecture and tutorial; Havens and Imre, pp. 25-41

## Derived Short-Answer Practice

> How does Budapest use location as soft power?

**Answer:** Rebates, skilled crews, adaptable architecture and trusted intermediaries make foreign producers want to work there. The attraction changes industry relationships without direct coercion, even when Budapest plays another city on screen.
