# Media-Service Industry

**Unit:** Unit 7: Location as Soft Power
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Memorise This

A media-service industry provides locations, labour, facilities and production support for projects controlled or financed by outside companies.

## Detailed Definition

A media-service industry provides locations, labour, facilities and production support for projects controlled or financed by outside companies.

## Key Terms

- runaway production
- locations
- skilled labour
- facilities
- foreign production

## Key Points

- tax incentives and lower costs attract mobile productions
- local workers may gain employment, skills and industrial capacity
- foreign companies may retain ownership and higher-value control
- outcomes vary: Hungary has relatively durable service work, while other locations may experience precarity or weaker domestic production

## PPT Case

**Case:** Foreign productions filmed in Budapest

**Why it fits:** Local crews and facilities service externally controlled projects, creating work alongside dependency.
