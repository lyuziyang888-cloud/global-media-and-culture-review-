# Place-Making

**Unit:** Unit 7: Location as Soft Power
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Memorise This

Place-making is the process through which institutions, industries and media give locations particular meanings and identities.

## Detailed Definition

Place-making is the process through which institutions, industries and media give locations particular meanings and identities.

## Key Terms

- constructed place
- representation
- audience
- purpose
- changing identity

## Key Points

- places are not represented neutrally
- governments, producers, fixers and audiences may construct different versions
- a location may represent itself or stand in for somewhere else
- the same city can carry several meanings at once

## PPT Case

**Case:** Budapest and Brisbane

**Why it fits:** Media and industry give each city selective meanings for different audiences and production purposes.
