# Fixers

**Unit:** Unit 7: Location as Soft Power
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Definition

Fixers are industry intermediaries who connect foreign producers with government agencies, local workers and other stakeholders. Some may also work in or with official institutions.

## What They Do

In Havens and Imre, fixers turn a national promotional claim into practical production. They interpret foreign producers' needs, identify locations and suppliers, negotiate with officials and reassure both sides. Their knowledge is relational: they know whom to contact and how to make different institutional expectations compatible.

## Key Terms

- intermediary
- negotiation
- translation
- reassurance
- cultural diplomacy

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Government agencies promote Hungary, while local fixers connect foreign producers with officials, crews and suppliers.
- These routine professional relationships turn a national image into workable production access.

## Why They Matter

The government can advertise Hungary as film-friendly, but a production still requires permits, crews, vendors and workable locations. Fixers assemble those connections and may present Budapest as Paris, Munich, Istanbul or itself according to the project. They therefore participate in place-making while also adapting or contradicting the state's preferred national image.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** What role do fixers play in Budapest's film industry?

**How to answer:** Identify the parties separated by an institutional or cultural gap and show the concrete coordination performed by the fixer.

**Do not overclaim:** Media-service production can create investment and expertise, but foreign projects can relocate and may leave local producers dependent or visually erase the location.
