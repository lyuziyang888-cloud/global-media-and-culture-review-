# Canned Programs

## 30-Second Definition

A canned program is produced and broadcast in one territory, then distributed to another territory for rebroadcast as a finished program.

## Keywords To Memorise

- finished program
- rebroadcast
- cross-border distribution
- limited adaptation

## Key Features

- transfers the completed content rather than production knowledge
- usually preserves the original cast, setting and performance
- may be dubbed or subtitled for another market
- differs from a format, which is locally reproduced and adapted

## Why It Matters

The distinction shows that international television trade can move either finished texts or the knowledge needed to reproduce a concept locally.

## Limitation

Calling a program canned does not mean reception is identical everywhere; audiences can still interpret the same text differently.

## Possible Exam Questions

- What is a canned program?
- Compare canned programs and format programs.
- Why might a broadcaster license a format rather than import a finished program?
