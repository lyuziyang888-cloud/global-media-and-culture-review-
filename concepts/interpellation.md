# Interpellation

## Unit Role

**Unit 3 core concept.** Althusser's concept explains how social structures recruit people into identity positions.

## 30-Second Definition

Interpellation is the process through which a person is socially "hailed" and recognises an offered identity position as referring to them.

## Keywords To Memorise

- Althusser
- hailing
- recognition
- recruitment
- identity position
- ideology

## Key Features

- Social institutions and representations offer identity positions.
- Interpellation succeeds when a person responds, in effect, "that is me."
- Recognition involves participation by the individual, not only external labelling.
- Available positions are shaped by wider cultural and ideological structures.

## Why It Matters

Interpellation links the personal experience of recognition to the social production of identities. It helps explain why imposed categories can feel natural or personally meaningful.

## Relationships

- Interpellation is one mechanism of [Identification](identification.md).
- It demonstrates the interaction of [Structure and Agency](structure-and-agency.md).
- Advertising and [Representation](representation.md) can hail audiences into roles.

## Example

Woodward discusses advertising that invites women to recognise domestic motherhood as the identity that fits them.
