# Multiple Identities

## Definition

Multiple identities are the different, overlapping and sometimes conflicting identities a person holds across nationality, gender, class, work, family, sexuality, ethnicity and other social positions.

## Keywords

- multiplicity
- overlap
- contradiction
- context
- continuity
- fluidity

## Core Understanding

- A person can belong to several groups simultaneously.
- Different identities become more important in different contexts.
- Identities can conflict rather than form a perfectly unified whole.
- Institutional categories often simplify this complexity.
- The concept prevents identity from being reduced to a single label and helps explain why official classifications cannot fully represent lived experience.

## Possible Questions

- Why do people have multiple identities?
- Can multiple identities conflict?
- What are the limits of official identity categories?
