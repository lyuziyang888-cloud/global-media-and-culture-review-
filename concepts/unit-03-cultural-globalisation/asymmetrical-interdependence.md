# Asymmetrical Interdependence

**Unit:** Unit 3: Cultural Globalisation

## Definition

Asymmetrical interdependence describes relationships in which countries, industries or cultures depend on and influence one another, but not with equal resources, control or effects.

## Keywords

- connection
- mutual dependence
- inequality
- influence
- complexity
- power

## Core Understanding

- The concept avoids two extremes: imagining completely separate cultures or assuming equal global exchange. It allows multidirectional interaction and structural inequality to be analysed together.
- Media imperialism emphasises the asymmetry; cultural globalisation emphasises the interdependence and interaction.
- Local producers may supply culturally specific content to global platforms while depending on those platforms for investment and distribution.

## Possible Questions

- What is asymmetrical interdependence?
- How does it provide a middle ground between the two paradigms?
- Can interdependence remain unequal?
