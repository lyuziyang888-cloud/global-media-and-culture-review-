# Media Capacity

**Unit:** Unit 6: Media Capacity
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Memorise This

Media capacity is the lasting ability to create, finance, own, improve and circulate media through local skills, infrastructure, knowledge and intellectual property.

## Detailed Definition

Media capacity is the lasting ability to create, finance, own, improve and circulate media through local skills, infrastructure, knowledge and intellectual property.

## Key Terms

- production capability
- intellectual property
- skilled labour
- infrastructure
- innovation

## Key Points

- international visibility alone does not prove capacity
- low-cost outsourcing may build some skills while leaving ownership elsewhere
- lasting capacity enables repeated production and movement into higher-value activities
- control and continuing dependency must both be evaluated

## PPT Case

**Case:** Seoul Digital Media City and East Asian media industries

**Why it fits:** The cases allow evaluation of whether global integration leaves lasting local skills, infrastructure, knowledge and IP control.
