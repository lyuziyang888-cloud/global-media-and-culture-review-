# Keane's Five Models

**Unit:** Unit 6: Media Capacity

## Definition

Keane's framework identifies five ways once-peripheral media industries may participate in and develop through the global economy.

## Five Models

1. **World factory:** low-cost outsourced work; local skills may grow, but foreign firms often retain IP.
2. **Mimetic isomorphism:** imitation of successful formats or formulas; it may work briefly but creates originality and IP risks.
3. **Cultural technology transfer:** co-production, licensing and franchises share expertise or IP.
4. **Niche breakthrough markets:** distinctive content uses expanding channels to reach wider audiences.
5. **Creative/industrial clusters:** co-located firms, infrastructure and talent exchange knowledge and support innovation.

## Core Understanding

The categories are analytical models, not guaranteed stages. The fifth provides the strongest link to lasting capacity-building, while the others may still create opportunities and limitations.

## Possible Questions

- Define and compare Keane's five categories.
- Which category best demonstrates lasting media capacity?
- Apply two categories to a media company.
