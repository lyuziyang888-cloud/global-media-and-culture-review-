# Streaming Localisation

**Unit:** Unit 5: Localising Streaming

## Definition

Streaming localisation refers to strategies used by platforms to adapt content, pricing, language, genres and business practices to particular markets and audiences.

## Keywords

- market adaptation
- language
- pricing
- local production
- audience preferences

## Core Understanding

- localisation existed before streaming, as shown by Star TV and MTV
- filming locally is not enough to make content culturally local
- platforms may use subtitles, dubbing, regional languages and local partners
- localisation strategies vary according to market power and cultural diversity

## Possible Questions

- Why do streaming platforms localise?
- Is locally produced content necessarily local in cultural or industrial terms?
