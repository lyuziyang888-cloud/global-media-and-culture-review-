# Bollywoodization of Netflix

**Unit:** Unit 5: Localising Streaming
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Definition

The Bollywoodization of Netflix describes Netflix's increasing dependence on Bollywood stars, directors, production companies, genres and aesthetics when localising in India.

## Mechanism In The Reading

Punathambekar and Mohan show that Netflix could not enter India using technology and global capital alone. It recruited recognised Hindi-film actors, directors and production companies because these networks supplied creative labour, reputation and knowledge of Indian audiences. “Bollywoodization” therefore describes a global platform being changed by an established local industry.

## Key Terms

- Bollywood
- local partners
- star power
- Sacred Games
- production networks

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Netflix relied on Indian story material, Bollywood talent and local production knowledge for Sacred Games.
- It promoted the series as India’s Narcos, combining local content with a globally recognisable crime-drama frame.

## How *Sacred Games* Demonstrates It

Netflix adapted Vikram Chandra's Indian novel through Indian production networks and prominent Bollywood talent. Promotion as “India's *Narcos*” made the crime series legible within Netflix's global brand, while its story, performers and production knowledge came from India. The case combines global genre packaging with dependence on local industrial resources; it is not simply a US platform imposing foreign content.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** Does Netflix dominate India on its own terms?

**How to answer:** Identify the Indian resource Netflix required, explain how it altered Netflix's strategy, then note what control Netflix still retained.

**Do not overclaim:** Local adaptation and competition constrain global platforms, but they do not remove their financial, technological and distribution advantages.

**Course basis:** Punathambekar and Mohan, “Bollywoodizing Netflix or globalizing Hotstar?”; Week 5 lecture, slides 14-17.

## Possible Short Answer

> How did *Sacred Games* show the Bollywoodization of Netflix?

**Answer:** Netflix used Indian stories but also relied on Bollywood actors, directors, companies and production knowledge. Its entry into India therefore depended on an established local industry rather than a purely imported strategy.
