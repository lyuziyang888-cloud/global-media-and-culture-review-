# Industrial Contact Zones

**Unit:** Unit 5: Localising Streaming
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Definition

Industrial contact zones are spaces where global and local media industries meet, interact and change within power relations that may be unequal but are not fixed.

## Mechanism

Punathambekar and Mohan adapt the contact-zone idea to industrial relations. Global platforms enter a market with capital and technology, but encounter local producers, stars, languages, regulation, competitors and audience habits. Interaction changes both sides. Power remains unequal, yet its outcome must be investigated in the particular market rather than assumed in advance.

## Key Terms

- global-local interaction
- negotiation
- asymmetrical power
- local agency
- market context

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Hotstar organised its service around the IPL, Bollywood and Indian-language programming.
- The IPL attracted domestic, diasporic and transnational audiences; the reading reports more than 700 million worldwide viewers in 2018.
- The reading reported about 25 million Hotstar subscribers in India compared with 4.6 million for Netflix.
- Global scale therefore did not automatically give Netflix dominance in the Indian market.

## Why India Is A Contact Zone

Netflix brought global scale but adapted through Bollywood labour and stories. Hotstar combined global streaming technology with cricket, Hindi cinema and regional-language content. The reading reported roughly 25 million Hotstar subscribers in India against 4.6 million for Netflix, showing that Netflix's worldwide size did not automatically produce Indian market dominance. Local industrial strength constrained and redirected the global entrant.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** Why is India an industrial contact zone for streaming platforms?

**How to answer:** Name both sets of actors, show what each contributes, identify how each changes and compare their bargaining power with evidence.

**Do not overclaim:** Local adaptation and competition constrain global platforms, but they do not remove their financial, technological and distribution advantages.

## Possible Short Answer

> Why is Indian streaming an industrial contact zone?

**Answer:** Global platform strategies meet powerful Indian film industries, languages, pricing conditions and cricket culture. Netflix and Hotstar are changed by this encounter, although they do not enter it with equal resources.
