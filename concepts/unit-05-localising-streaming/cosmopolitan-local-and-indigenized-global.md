# Cosmopolitan Local and Indigenized Global

**Unit:** Unit 5: Localising Streaming

## Definition

The cosmopolitan local uses local stories and talent within genres designed for globally oriented audiences. The indigenized global adapts global industrial practices around the priorities of a specific local market and its audiences.

## Keywords

- cosmopolitan local
- indigenized global
- Netflix
- Hotstar
- global audience
- local audience

## Core Understanding

- Netflix initially used cosmopolitan-local strategies in India
- Hotstar built an indigenized-global strategy around Indian languages, Bollywood and cricket
- neither strategy is completely global or completely local
- comparing them shows how platforms negotiate different market conditions
