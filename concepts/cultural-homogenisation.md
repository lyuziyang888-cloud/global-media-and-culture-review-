# Cultural Homogenisation

## Definition

Cultural homogenisation is the process through which cultural differences are reduced as dominant media, values, styles and standards spread across societies.

## Keywords

- sameness
- dominant values
- reduced diversity
- standardisation
- Americanisation
- local culture

## Core Understanding

- Dominant media can normalise similar tastes, lifestyles and consumer values.
- Foreign standards can displace or marginalise local practices.
- Homogenisation is a possible outcome, not an automatic audience effect.
- Local interpretation, resistance and successful non-US industries limit the claim.
- It identifies the cultural consequence feared by media and cultural imperialism, while also creating a point for criticism: circulation does not prove identical reception.

## Possible Questions

- Does media imperialism necessarily cause cultural homogenisation?
- How can audiences and local industries resist homogenisation?
