# Identity Crisis

## Definition

An identity crisis is uncertainty about who people are or which roles remain available when established identities are disrupted by social change.

## Keywords

- uncertainty
- social change
- globalisation
- continuity
- diversity
- new opportunities

## Core Understanding

- Economic restructuring can weaken occupational and community identities.
- Changing gender, family and national roles can disrupt established expectations.
- Globalisation can increase both diversity and uncertainty.
- Change creates new identity possibilities as well as loss.
- The concept connects personal uncertainty to wider historical change. It prevents identity problems from being treated only as individual psychological issues.

## Possible Questions

- What causes an identity crisis?
- How does social change affect identity?
- Does greater identity diversity create freedom or uncertainty?
