# Globalisation

**Unit:** Unit 1: Global Media
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Definition

Globalisation refers to the increasing interconnectedness of people, economies, cultures, technologies and media across national borders.

## What This Concept Explains

Globalisation is the growing connection and movement of media and culture across national borders.

## Key Terms

- interconnectedness
- cross-border flows
- media circulation
- cultural exchange
- technology
- global market
- local culture

## How the Evidence Shows the Concept

Globalisation is important because it explains how media industries, audiences and cultures are connected beyond one nation. It helps explain why a Canadian production, a Korean-themed film, Netflix, HBO and local cultural policy can all be part of the same media question.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** Explain the relationship between globalisation and local culture.

**Answer order:** definition -> relevant evidence above -> explain the connection -> limitation -> judgement.

**Do not overclaim:** Global circulation does not automatically erase local identity, but visibility through a global platform does not give local producers full control over funding or circulation.
