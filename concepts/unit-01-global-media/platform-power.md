# Platform Power

**Unit:** Unit 1: Global Media
**Exam priority:** CORE - know the definition and one case

## Definition

Platform power refers to the ability of digital platforms to influence which content is funded, distributed, promoted and accessed by audiences.

## Mechanism

A platform gains power by controlling a route between producers and audiences. It can decide availability, territorial access and promotion, while its scale makes that route difficult for smaller producers to replace. Week 1 introduces this through distribution; Unit 2 later develops the political-economic explanation.

## Key Terms

- distribution power
- visibility
- funding
- audience access
- algorithms
- market power
- regulation

## Concrete Course Evidence

- The project originated with Canadian platform Crave.
- Its source author Rachel Reid is from Nova Scotia, and its ice-hockey subject matter carries Canadian cultural associations.
- Canadian creators, cast, crew, locations and imagery make it locally identifiable, while HBO provides US circulation.
- The series is set in an Inuit community in the Canadian Arctic.
- Indigenous Canadians have substantial roles in its creation, performance and production.
- CBC and APTN connect it to Canadian and Indigenous media institutions; Netflix gives it international distribution.
- The Week 1 tutorial requires nationality to be assessed through source material, creators, cast, crew, funding, ownership, locations, story and distribution.
- A production can therefore be locally rooted and globally circulated at the same time.

## How The Cases Demonstrate It

Netflix allows *North of North* to reach viewers beyond Canada, while HBO gives *Heated Rivalry* access to the US market. The local producers supply the culturally specific programs, but the international services control important distribution channels. The CRTC levy debate makes that power measurable: Canadian regulators attempted to redirect part of streamer revenue into domestic and Indigenous production (Stevis-Gridneff, 2026, pp. 1-2).


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** How does platform power affect local media?

**How to answer:** State what the platform controls, what opportunity that provides and what dependency it creates.

**Do not overclaim:** Global circulation does not automatically erase local identity, but visibility through a global platform does not give local producers full control over funding or circulation.

**Course basis:** Stevis-Gridneff (2026), PDF pp. 1-2; Week 1 tutorial.

## Possible Short Answer

> What power does Netflix have over local productions?

**Answer:** Netflix can provide finance, international distribution and access to audiences, but it also influences availability and visibility. A local production may gain reach without gaining full control over how it circulates.
