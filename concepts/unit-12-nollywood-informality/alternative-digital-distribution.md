# Alternative Digital Distribution

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Alternative digital distribution moves digital files through methods suited to local infrastructure instead of continuous platform streaming. Miller (2021, pp. 264-268) includes Bluetooth, FlashShare, USB, kiosks, compressed downloads and subscription-based person-to-person transfer.

## Course Evidence And Application

Rural market stalls transferred low-cost films directly to phones. iROKOtv tested kiosks and later rights-managed sharing that required a subscription but almost no mobile data. These methods answered expensive data, unreliable electricity and limited online payment. Digital files therefore circulated without a Netflix-style stream.

## Exam Use

**Short:** Nollywood used phone transfers, kiosks and low-data sharing to fit local infrastructure.

**Long:** Name the infrastructure problem, describe the adapted route, then evaluate access, rights and control.

**Limit:** Adaptation does not remove piracy, incomplete records or dependence on investors.

**Source:** Miller (2021), pp. 264-268.
