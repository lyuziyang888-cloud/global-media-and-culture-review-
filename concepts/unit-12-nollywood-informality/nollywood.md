# Nollywood

**Unit:** Unit 12 Nollywood Informality
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Memorise This

Nigeria's film industry. It developed mainly through straight-to-video production and distribution rather than theatrical release.

## Definition

Nigeria's film industry. It developed mainly through straight-to-video production and distribution rather than theatrical release.

## Key Terms

Nigeria; local content; straight-to-video; low budget; high volume

## Key Points

Nollywood does not follow one language, city or Western industrial model. Physical copies, informal networks and local audience demand remain central even as streaming, satellite television and cinema expand.

## PPT Case

**Case:** Straight-to-video Nollywood production

**Why it fits:** The industry developed through local demand and physical distribution rather than following the Hollywood theatrical model.
