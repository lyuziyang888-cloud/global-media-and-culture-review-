# Nollywood

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Nollywood is Nigeria's film industry, historically built through fast, low-budget production and informal physical distribution rather than a Hollywood-style studio and cinema system. Miller (2021, pp. 259-262) shows why it cannot be judged by streaming alone.

## Course Evidence And Application

Open-air markets and physical copies remained central to domestic circulation. Marketers financed films, acted as executive producers and controlled sales information. Formal television and streaming later developed alongside these networks rather than simply replacing them. Nollywood's scale came from locally adapted finance and distribution as well as film output.

## Exam Use

## Derived Short-Answer Practice

> Why is Nollywood important to global media study?

**Answer:** It became a large industry through locally adapted production, marketers and physical distribution rather than copying the Hollywood cinema model. It demonstrates that media capacity can develop through a different industrial system.

**Long:** Explain why the industry matters, then connect marketers, infrastructure and mixed formal/informal distribution.

**Limit:** Informality creates access but can obscure revenue and concentrate distributor power.

**Source:** Miller (2021), pp. 259-276.
