# Nollywood

**Unit:** Unit 12 Nollywood Informality
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Definition

Nigeria's film industry. It developed mainly through straight-to-video production and distribution rather than theatrical release.

## What This Concept Explains

Nigeria's film industry. It developed mainly through straight-to-video production and distribution rather than theatrical release.

## Key Terms

Nigeria; local content; straight-to-video; low budget; high volume

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Nollywood marketers finance films, act as executive producers and control physical distribution through open-air markets.
- They conceal sales figures and their collective networks have blocked some large-scale attempts to replace them.
- Miller describes rural stalls charging phones and transferring inexpensive unauthorised films through Bluetooth or FlashShare.
- The system uses little data and limited electricity, so digital circulation occurs without continuous broadband streaming.
- Streaming one film could cost about US$5 in data, alongside electricity and payment barriers.
- Netflix retained credit-card payment and data-heavy streaming while giving limited emphasis to Nigerian content.

## How the Evidence Shows the Concept

Nollywood does not follow one language, city or Western industrial model. Physical copies, informal networks and local audience demand remain central even as streaming, satellite television and cinema expand.
