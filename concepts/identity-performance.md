# Identity Performance

## Unit Role

**Unit 3 core concept.** Goffman's approach explains how identity is presented and interpreted in everyday interaction.

## 30-Second Definition

Identity performance is the presentation of self through speech, dress, behaviour and interaction, using social roles and scripts that others interpret.

## Keywords To Memorise

- Goffman
- presentation of self
- role
- script
- impression management
- everyday interaction

## Key Features

- Social interaction resembles a performance in which people take up roles.
- Existing cultural scripts guide how roles should be performed.
- People manage the impressions they give to others.
- Unintended behaviour may communicate meanings that contradict the intended performance.

## Why It Matters

Performance connects personal agency with social expectations. People can interpret a role, but they do not invent its scripts without social constraints.

## Relationships

- Performance makes [Identity](identity.md) visible in interaction.
- [Structure and Agency](structure-and-agency.md) explains the balance between existing scripts and individual interpretation.
- [Representation](representation.md) supplies images and meanings used in self-presentation.

## Example

Clothing choices can present a smart, professional, rebellious or casual identity and affect how others interpret the wearer.
