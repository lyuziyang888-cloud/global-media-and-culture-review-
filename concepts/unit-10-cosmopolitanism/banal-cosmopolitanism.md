# Banal Cosmopolitanism

## Definition

The ordinary, often non-reflexive experience of global culture through everyday consumption.

## Keywords

Everyday media, ordinary consumption, foreign content, non-reflexive globality.

## Core Understanding

Foreign content becomes a normal part of daily viewing. Audiences may learn about another country mainly from what appears on screen without actively researching it.

## Possible Questions

- How can television create banal cosmopolitanism?
- How does banal cosmopolitanism differ from pop cosmopolitanism?
