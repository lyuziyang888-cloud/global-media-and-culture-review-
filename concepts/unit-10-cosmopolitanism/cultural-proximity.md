# Cultural Proximity

**Unit:** Unit 10 Cosmopolitanism
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Definition

The idea that audiences tend to prefer media that feels culturally familiar through language, values, genre, religion or everyday experience.

## What This Concept Explains

The idea that audiences tend to prefer media that feels culturally familiar through language, values, genre, religion or everyday experience.

## Key Terms

Familiarity, shared values, language, genre, audience preference.

## Concrete Course Evidence

- Turkish drama entered a Spanish market already familiar with Latin American telenovelas; Rosa moved from Mexican telenovelas to Turkish series.
- Familiar melodrama helped entry, but interviewees also described cultural difference as an attraction.

## How the Evidence Shows the Concept

Cultural proximity explains some cross-border viewing, but it cannot explain every case. Spanish viewers also valued the novelty and difference of Turkish dramas.


## Exam Use

**Possible long question:** Why is cultural proximity an incomplete explanation of foreign-media popularity?

**Answer order:** definition -> relevant evidence above -> explain the connection -> limitation -> judgement.

**Do not overclaim:** Watching foreign media does not automatically create cosmopolitan openness. Industrial access, audience background and the reproduction of stereotypes must also be considered.
