# Cultural Proximity

**Unit:** Unit 10 Cosmopolitanism
**Exam priority:** HIGH - memorise and practise applying it

## Memorise This

The idea that audiences tend to prefer media that feels culturally familiar through language, values, genre, religion or everyday experience.

## Detailed Definition

The idea that audiences tend to prefer media that feels culturally familiar through language, values, genre, religion or everyday experience.

## Key Terms

Familiarity, shared values, language, genre, audience preference.

## Key Points

Cultural proximity explains some cross-border viewing, but it cannot explain every case. Spanish viewers also valued the novelty and difference of Turkish dramas.

## PPT Case

**Case:** Spanish audiences watching Turkish dramas

**Why it fits:** Familiar melodrama and shared values help attraction, but novelty and cultural difference also matter.
