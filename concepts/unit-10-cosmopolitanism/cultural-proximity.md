# Cultural Proximity

**Priority:** HIGH

## Definition And Source

Cultural proximity means audiences often prefer media that feels culturally familiar.

## Course Evidence And Application

Spanish audiences already knew melodrama through Latin American telenovelas; Rosa moved from Mexican telenovelas to Turkish dramas (p. 941). Familiar family conflict and romance made the programs readable, while unfamiliar Turkish places and customs also attracted viewers. Proximity and difference worked together.

## Exam Use

## Derived Short-Answer Practice

> Why did cultural proximity only partly explain Turkish dramas' appeal in Spain?

**Answer:** Familiar melodrama and family themes made the programs accessible, but viewers were also attracted by unfamiliar Turkish places and customs. Familiarity and difference worked together.

**Long:** Use proximity for initial accessibility and cosmopolitanism for interest in difference.

**Limit:** The concept cannot explain all foreign-media success or assume one national taste.

**Source:** Ferreira (2026), pp. 933-934, 941.
