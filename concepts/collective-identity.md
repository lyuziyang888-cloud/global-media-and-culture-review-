# Collective Identity

## Unit Role

**Unit 3 supporting concept.** Collective identity explains how shared identification can support political action and social change.

## 30-Second Definition

Collective identity is a shared sense of "we" through which people recognise common experiences, interests or differences and act together.

## Keywords To Memorise

- shared identity
- belonging
- social movements
- collective action
- resistance
- reconstruction

## Key Features

- Collective identity turns personal experience into shared meaning.
- It can provide solidarity for groups facing exclusion.
- Social movements can reconstruct negative representations.
- Collective action can influence the structures that shape identity.

## Why It Matters

Collective identity shows how agency can operate beyond individual choice. Groups can challenge stereotypes, claim recognition and reshape available identities.

## Relationships

- Collective identity develops through [Identification](identification.md).
- It can celebrate [Difference](difference.md) rather than treating it as inferiority.
- It demonstrates collective agency within [Structure and Agency](structure-and-agency.md).

## Example

Woodward describes gay rights movements transforming "gay" into a more positive representation of sexual identity.
