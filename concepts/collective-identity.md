# Collective Identity

**Unit:** Identity Material: Unit Pending Confirmation

## Definition

Collective identity is a shared sense of "we" through which people recognise common experiences, interests or differences and act together.

## Keywords

- shared identity
- belonging
- social movements
- collective action
- resistance
- reconstruction

## Core Understanding

- Collective identity turns personal experience into shared meaning.
- It can provide solidarity for groups facing exclusion.
- Social movements can reconstruct negative representations.
- Collective action can influence the structures that shape identity.
- Collective identity shows how agency can operate beyond individual choice. Groups can challenge stereotypes, claim recognition and reshape available identities.

## Possible Questions

- What is collective identity?
- How can social movements reconstruct identity?
- Explain the relationship between collective identity and agency.
