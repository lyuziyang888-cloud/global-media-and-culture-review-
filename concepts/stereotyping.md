# Stereotyping

**Unit:** Identity Material: Unit Pending Confirmation

## Definition

Stereotyping is the repeated reduction of a group to simplified, generalised characteristics that appear natural and can reinforce unequal power relations.

## Keywords

- simplification
- repetition
- categorisation
- representation
- inequality
- exclusion

## Core Understanding

- Stereotypes treat diverse individuals as if they share a fixed identity.
- They exaggerate selected characteristics and ignore complexity.
- Repetition can make constructed categories seem natural.
- Stereotypes may be challenged, subverted or reclaimed.
- Stereotyping explains how identities can be produced by others and how representation connects difference to social inequality.

## Possible Questions

- How do stereotypes construct identity?
- Why are stereotypes connected to power?
- How can stereotyped groups challenge imposed identities?
