# Body Projects

## Unit Role

**Unit 3 supporting concept.** Body projects show how the body can become a site for constructing and contesting identity.

## 30-Second Definition

Body projects are deliberate practices through which people shape, present or reinterpret their bodies as part of identity construction.

## Keywords To Memorise

- embodiment
- self-construction
- gender
- age
- stereotype
- agency and constraint

## Key Features

- Bodies carry social meanings about gender, age, sexuality and ability.
- People can use bodily practices to challenge expectations.
- Biological and material limits still constrain bodily agency.
- Representations influence which bodies appear normal or desirable.

## Why It Matters

Body projects make the structure-agency relationship concrete: individuals can reconstruct embodied identity, but not under conditions of unlimited choice.

## Relationships

- Body projects are practices of [Identity](identity.md) construction.
- They demonstrate [Structure and Agency](structure-and-agency.md).
- They can resist [Stereotyping](stereotyping.md) and dominant [Representation](representation.md).

## Example

Woodward discusses women bodybuilders who challenge assumptions about appropriate feminine bodies and an Age Concern image that contests stereotypes of older women.

## Possible Exam Questions

- What is a body project?
- How can the body be used to construct identity?
- Do body projects demonstrate freedom or social constraint?

## Source Anchor
