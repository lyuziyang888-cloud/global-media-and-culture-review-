# Week 12 Tutorial: Nollywood

## Main Focus

Use Nollywood to challenge the assumption that digital distribution must mean formal streaming platforms.

## Official Exam Practice

### Discuss how Nollywood offers an alternative understanding of digital distribution compared to the dominant models assumed in the Global North. Be sure to include the role of marketers in your response.

Nollywood uses marketers, market stalls and file transfers instead of relying only on streaming. This improves access but can weaken creators' control.

## Discussion Questions and Short Answers

### 1. What do these media systems offer their audiences that conventional media services might not?

They provide affordable local access where cinemas or streaming services are limited.

### 2. How do these examples challenge typical understandings about piracy and distribution? Are there problems with these systems?

They show that piracy can increase access, but creators may lose payment and control.

### 3. Who gets to decide what is global?

Large platforms and distributors have the most power to label content as global.

### 4. Why are shows like *The Black Book* and *Squid Game* celebrated as breakthroughs?

Netflix gave them worldwide visibility. However, their local industries existed before Netflix recognised them.

### 5. Does “global success” mean the same thing to Netflix, creatives, and audiences?

No. Netflix values subscriptions, creators value funding and control, and audiences value accessible stories.

### 6. How does “global success” influence the stories that get made?

Producers may make stories that platforms believe can travel globally. Local stories may receive less attention.

## How Should You Study?

Identify the Global North assumption, explain Nollywood's alternative and give one benefit and one problem.
