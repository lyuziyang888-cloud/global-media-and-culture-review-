## Discussion Questions and Suggested Answers

### What position does Netflix hold in India?

Netflix is influential but not dominant. It competes with strong services and must adapt pricing, genres, partnerships and content to India's scale, linguistic diversity, government policy and established film industry.

### What does India's streaming market tell us about global media?

Global platform power depends on local context. Ownership and capital matter, but local industries, audiences, regulation and competitors can reshape platform strategy.

### What is the value of studying Netflix comparatively?

Comparing countries prevents Netflix from being treated as one identical global force and reveals how its power changes across markets.
