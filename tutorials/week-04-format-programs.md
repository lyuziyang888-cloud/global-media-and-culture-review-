# Week 4 Tutorial: Format Programs

## Main Focus

Explain how a licensed format keeps a stable core while adapting to local audiences.

## Workbook 1 Discussion

### 1. What position (CI/MI or CG paradigm) did you take?

I chose cultural globalisation because the report showed media moving in several directions.

### 2. What is the evidence that supports your position?

The report showed increasing investment in non-US content. This supports multidirectional media flow.

### 3. Can you come to an agreement as a group on which position better explains the global media industry today? Why did your group agree/disagree?

I choose cultural globalisation because it better explains the growth of non-US production. Platform ownership remains a limitation.

## Format-Program Questions

### 4. Is each format program unique content or should we consider them as essentially the same?

They are both similar and different. The rules stay the same, but the cast, language and setting change.

### 5. Do you think format program trade challenges the West vs Rest thinking of the world?

Yes. *The Traitors* moved from the Netherlands to many countries, although the format owner still controls the licence.

## How Should You Study?

Remember: stable licensed core plus local adaptation.
