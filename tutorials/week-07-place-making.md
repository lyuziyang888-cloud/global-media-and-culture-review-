# Week 7 Tutorial: Place-Making

## Tutorial Purpose

Compare how media, tourism and industry promotion construct Budapest and Brisbane for different audiences.

## Key Concepts and Clarifications

- place is constructed rather than fixed
- different institutions select different images
- a location can represent itself or another place

## How Should You Study?

Practise identifying who constructs a place, the intended audience, the image being created and what that image leaves out.

## Tutorial Exam Practice

### Discuss how place is understood similarly in media capitals and Hollywood spy thrillers filmed in Hungary.

**Answer:** Both approaches treat place as constructed and changeable rather than fixed.

1. A media capital gains or loses status through history, institutions, talent and industry connections.
2. In spy thrillers, Budapest can appear as a modern destination while also standing in for other European cities.
3. In both cases, a place's meaning depends on changing industrial relationships and representation.

## Discussion Questions and Suggested Answers

### Budapest

### Do the examples construct the same Budapest?

No. The Parliament presents a monumental national capital, the arcade suggests a commercial European city, and the comedy *Budapest* presents an entertaining tourist destination. Each selects a different image for a different purpose.

### Who defines Budapest?

Government agencies, fixers, foreign producers, local workers and media texts all contribute. Their influence is unequal, so no single image is neutral or complete.

### Brisbane

### How is Brisbane constructed differently?

- **Visit Brisbane:** a desirable place for tourists, centred on leisure, food, attractions and accessible urban precincts.
- **Ausfilm:** a versatile and production-ready location for international screen companies.
- ***Boy Swallows Universe* coverage:** a specific, unequal and historically grounded 1980s Brisbane shaped by suburbs, class, migration and memory.

### Who is the intended audience?

Visit Brisbane addresses visitors; Ausfilm addresses producers and investors; the ABC article addresses audiences interested in the story, production and local identity.

### Does Brisbane need to remain Brisbane?

It matters strongly in *Boy Swallows Universe* because place shapes the story and characters. For service-production promotion, Brisbane may be more valuable when it can also represent other locations.

### Which version reaches the largest audience?

The screen version may gain the widest international reach through Netflix, but the tourism and industry versions directly target people who can visit or bring productions to Brisbane. Reach and practical influence are therefore not the same.
