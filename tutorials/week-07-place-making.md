# Week 7 Tutorial: Place-Making

## Main Focus

Identify who constructs a place, for which audience and with what image.

## Official Exam Practice

### Discuss how place is understood similarly in the concept of media capital and in Hollywood spy thrillers filmed in Hungary.

Both show that place is not fixed. Industry networks make a media capital, while films can make Budapest represent another city.

## Discussion Questions and Short Answers

### 1. What is the impression each image gives you of Budapest?

The images present Budapest as historic, elegant or entertaining.

### 2. Do they all construct the same Budapest?

No. Each example presents Budapest differently for a different purpose.

### 3. Who gets to define what Budapest is?

Government agencies, producers and local workers define Budapest, but they have different levels of power.

### 4. How is Brisbane described in your example? What kind of place is it?

Visit Brisbane shows a tourist city, Ausfilm shows a filming location, and *Boy Swallows Universe* shows 1980s Brisbane.

### 5. Who seems to be the intended audience?

Visit Brisbane targets tourists, Ausfilm targets producers, and the ABC story targets television audiences.

### 6. Does Brisbane matter as Brisbane, or not so much? Why do you think so?

Brisbane matters in *Boy Swallows Universe* because it shapes the story. In other productions, it may represent another place.

### 7. Who gets to define Brisbane?

Tourism bodies, screen agencies and producers define Brisbane for different audiences.

### 8. Why do these groups define Brisbane differently?

They have different goals. Some attract tourists, while others attract productions or tell stories.

### 9. Which version of Brisbane is likely to reach the largest audience?

Netflix may reach the largest audience. Tourism and industry promotion target smaller, specific audiences.

## How Should You Study?

For each case, identify the creator, audience, image and what has been excluded.
