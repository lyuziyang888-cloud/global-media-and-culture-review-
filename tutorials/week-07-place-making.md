# Week 7 Tutorial: Place-Making

## Main Focus

Identify who constructs a place, for which audience and with what image.

## Official Exam Practice

### How is place understood similarly in media capitals and Hungarian spy thrillers?

Both treat place as constructed rather than fixed. A city gains media-capital status through industry connections, while film can make Budapest represent another city.

## Discussion Questions and Short Answers

### 1. Do the examples construct the same Budapest?

No. Government, tourism and fictional media select different images of the city for different purposes.

### 2. Who defines Budapest?

Government agencies, foreign producers and local workers all contribute, but they do not have equal power.

### 3. How is Brisbane constructed differently?

Visit Brisbane presents a tourist destination, Ausfilm presents a production location, and *Boy Swallows Universe* presents a specific 1980s Brisbane.

### 4. Who is the intended audience?

Visit Brisbane targets tourists, Ausfilm targets producers, and the ABC story targets viewers interested in Brisbane and the series.

### 5. Does Brisbane need to remain Brisbane?

Yes in *Boy Swallows Universe*, because place shapes the story. In service production, Brisbane may be used to represent somewhere else.

### 6. Which version reaches the largest audience?

Netflix may provide the widest reach, while tourism and industry promotion target smaller groups with more direct practical influence.

## How Should You Study?

For each case, identify the creator, audience, image and what has been excluded.
