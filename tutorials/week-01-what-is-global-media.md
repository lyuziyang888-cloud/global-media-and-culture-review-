# Week 1 Tutorial: What Is Global Media?

## Tutorial Purpose

The tutorial introduces globalisation and asks students to test the categories "global," "local," "Canadian" and "Western" against real media cases.

## Main Cases

### Netflix Keynote

The tutorial asks what "global" means in Netflix's presentation and whether platform-led globalisation is historically new.

### Heated Rivalry

Students consider whether the series should be called American or Canadian by examining:

- its status as a Crave Original
- its source novel by a Nova Scotian author
- its ice-hockey subject matter
- production, location, creators, ownership and distribution

## Core Questions

1. What makes a media text Canadian?
2. How should local content be defined?
3. What makes content Australian?
4. Is *Heated Rivalry* Western media?
5. Who is counted as the West and who is excluded?
6. Can geographical territory alone explain global and local?

## How To Answer

```text
define the category
-> choose relevant criteria
-> apply several production and cultural dimensions
-> identify ambiguity
-> make a justified classification
```

## Assessment Signal

Week 1 tutorial participation is not graded. Graded critical engagement begins in Week 2.
