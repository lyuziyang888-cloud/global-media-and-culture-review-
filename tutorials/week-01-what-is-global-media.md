# Week 1 Tutorial: What Is Global Media?

## Main Focus

Use ownership, production, story, location and distribution to decide whether media is local, global, Canadian or Western.

## Discussion Questions and Short Answers

### 1. What makes a media text Canadian?

Canadian creators, stories and production can make a text Canadian. *Heated Rivalry* is Canadian content distributed internationally.

### 2. How should local content be defined?

Local content is connected to a place through its creators, story, language or production. Filming location alone is not enough.

### 3. What makes content Australian?

Australian creators, stories and production can make content Australian. Foreign distribution does not remove that identity.

### 4. Is *Heated Rivalry* Western media?

Yes, because it comes from Canada. More precisely, it is Canadian content with global circulation.

### 5. Who is counted as the West and who is excluded?

The West usually means North America and Western Europe. The category reflects political and historical power.

### 6. Can geographical territory alone explain global and local?

No. One production can involve several countries through funding, workers and distribution.

## How Should You Study?

For any media text, check ownership, production, story, location, distribution and audience before making a classification.
