# Week 1 Tutorial: What Is Global Media?

## Main Focus

Use ownership, production, story, location and distribution to decide whether media is local, global, Canadian or Western.

## Discussion Questions and Short Answers

### 1. What makes a media text Canadian?

Canadian creators, production, stories, funding or locations can give a text a Canadian identity. *Heated Rivalry* is Canadian-rooted media with international circulation.

### 2. How should local content be defined?

Local content has a strong connection to a place through creative control, production, language or story. Filming location alone is not enough.

### 3. What makes content Australian?

Australian creators, stories, institutions and cultural concerns can make content Australian. Foreign funding or distribution does not automatically remove that identity.

### 4. Is *Heated Rivalry* Western media?

It can be called Western because it comes from Canada and North American media institutions. However, "Canadian-rooted and globally circulated" is more precise.

### 5. Who is counted as the West and who is excluded?

The term usually centres North America and Western Europe. Its boundaries reflect history and power, so the category should be questioned rather than treated as neutral.

### 6. Can geographical territory alone explain global and local?

No. A production can involve funding, workers, stories and platforms from several countries.

## How Should You Study?

For any media text, check ownership, production, story, location, distribution and audience before making a classification.
