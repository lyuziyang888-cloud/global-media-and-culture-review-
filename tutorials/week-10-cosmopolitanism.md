# Week 10 Tutorial: Cosmopolitanism

## Main Focus

Explain why audiences may be attracted to cultural difference as well as cultural familiarity.

## Official Exam Practice

### Discuss how cosmopolitanism challenges the cultural proximity theory by using the example of Spanish audiences watching Turkish dramas. Be sure to define the underlined concepts in your response.

Cultural proximity explains familiar genres. Cosmopolitanism explains why Spanish viewers enjoyed learning about Turkish culture.

## Discussion Questions and Short Answers

### 1. Can you apply “banal” or “pop” cosmopolitanism to the article?

Regular viewing can show banal cosmopolitanism. Learning about Thailand or travelling there is closer to pop cosmopolitanism.

### 2. Why do people consume foreign content or culture?

They may enjoy familiar stories or want to experience cultural difference.

### 3. Does consuming foreign media make people more interested in another culture? Why or why not?

Sometimes. Some viewers studied Turkish or travelled, but others only watched the dramas.

### 4. Based on Q1 & Q2, what seems to be enough to count as being “cosmopolitan”?

Cosmopolitanism requires interest in and reflection on cultural difference. Simply watching foreign media may not be enough.

## How Should You Study?

Distinguish familiarity from difference, then use one Spanish-audience example and one limitation.
