# Unit 7: Location and Soft Power Evidence

## Budapest

**Use for:** Place-making; media capitals (U3)

**Evidence:** Tax rebates, studios, skilled crews and flexible locations attract foreign productions.

**What it proves:** Media infrastructure and industry networks can turn a city into a production centre.

## Government and Fixers

**Use for:** Soft power; media services

**Evidence:** Government agencies promote Hungary, while local fixers connect foreign producers with services.

**What it proves:** Production support and professional relationships can improve a country's international appeal.

## Brisbane

**Use for:** Place-making; representation (U1)

**Evidence:** Tourism campaigns, Ausfilm and television programs present Brisbane in different ways.

**What it proves:** Media can give the same place several different identities.

**Source:** Havens and Imre, pp. 25-41.
