# Unit 5: Localising Streaming Evidence

| Case | Use for | Key evidence | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotstar cricketverse | indigenised global; cultural proximity (U3) | IPL, Bollywood and Indian-language content | Local cultural strengths can support streaming success. |
| *Sacred Games* | cosmopolitan local; localisation | Indian story and talent use a global crime genre | Netflix combines local production with global genre styles. |
| Netflix versus Hotstar | industrial contact zones; platform imperialism (U2) | Reading reported 25m Hotstar and 4.6m Netflix users | Global scale does not guarantee local dominance. |

**Source:** Rai (2024), pp. 65-83.
