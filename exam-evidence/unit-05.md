# Unit 5: Localising Streaming Evidence

## Hotstar Cricketverse

**Use for:** Indigenised global; cultural proximity (U3)

**Evidence:** Hotstar built its service around the IPL, Bollywood and Indian-language content.

**What it proves:** A streaming service can succeed by adapting to familiar local interests.

## *Sacred Games*

**Use for:** Cosmopolitan local; localisation

**Evidence:** The series combines an Indian story and local talent with a globally familiar crime genre.

**What it proves:** Local content can use a global genre to reach both Indian and international audiences.

## Netflix versus Hotstar

**Use for:** Industrial contact zones; platform imperialism (U2)

**Evidence:** The reading reported 25 million Hotstar users and 4.6 million Netflix users.

**What it proves:** A global platform does not automatically dominate a local streaming market.

**Source:** Rai (2024), pp. 65-83.
