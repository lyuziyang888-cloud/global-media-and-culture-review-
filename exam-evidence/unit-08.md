# Unit 8: Nation Branding Evidence

## Costa Rica and Honduras

**Use for:** Adventurous foreigner; Othering

**Evidence:** Foreign visitors lead the adventure, while local culture becomes part of their experience.

**What it proves:** Nation branding can present a country mainly through the foreign visitor's viewpoint.

## Peru and El Salvador

**Use for:** Locals serving foreigners; Othering

**Evidence:** Local people, labour and culture are presented as services for foreign visitors.

**What it proves:** A campaign can turn local identity into a product for outsiders.

## Qatar

**Use for:** Nation branding; soft power (U7)

**Evidence:** The campaign invites visitors to discover Qatar through luxury, culture and adventure.

**What it proves:** Nation branding can attract visitors, but it may simplify the country's identity.

**Source:** Mino (2023), pp. 182-189.
