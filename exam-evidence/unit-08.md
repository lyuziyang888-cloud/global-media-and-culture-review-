# Unit 8: Nation Branding Evidence

| Case | Use for | Key evidence | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Costa Rica and Honduras | adventurous foreigner; Othering | Foreign visitors act; local culture becomes the experience | Branding can centre the foreign gaze. |
| Peru and El Salvador | locals serving foreigners; Othering | Local labour and culture are offered to outsiders | National identity can become a commercial resource. |
| Qatar | nation branding; soft power (U7) | Visitors discover luxury, culture and adventure | Attraction can improve reputation but simplify identity. |

**Source:** Mino (2023), pp. 182-189.
