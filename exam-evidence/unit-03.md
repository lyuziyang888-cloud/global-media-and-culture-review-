# Unit 3: Cultural Globalisation Evidence

## Reading Claim

Mirrlees presents media imperialism and cultural globalisation as competing but incomplete paradigms: one foregrounds structural inequality, while the other foregrounds multidirectional exchange, local adaptation and audience meaning-making.

## Best Examples

### Lucas versus Legrain

**Concepts:** media imperialism, cultural globalisation

George Lucas interprets US entertainment exports as Americanisation; Philippe Legrain interprets global circulation as exchange, freedom and cultural hybridity.

**Use:** Their disagreement shows that the same media flow can be interpreted either through unequal cultural power or through active cross-cultural exchange.

### Jane the Virgin

**Concepts:** contra-flow, adaptation

The Venezuelan telenovela *Juana la Virgen* was adapted in the US, South Korea, India and several other markets.

**Use:** The franchise challenges a simple US-to-rest model because media formats can originate outside the traditional core and travel in several directions.

### Chinese Microdramas

**Concepts:** contra-flow, platform power

Chinese microdramas reach overseas audiences through streaming platforms.

**Use:** Their popularity supports cultural globalisation by showing non-US circulation, but platform control means the flow is not necessarily economically equal.

**Source:** Mirrlees (2013), Chapter 1; Week 3 lecture and tutorial.
