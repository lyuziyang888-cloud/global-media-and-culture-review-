# Unit 3: Cultural Globalisation Evidence

| Case | Use for | Key evidence | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lucas versus Legrain | media imperialism (U2); cultural globalisation (U3) | Americanisation versus exchange and hybridity | The same flow can be read as domination or exchange. |
| *Jane the Virgin* | contra-flow (U3); adaptation (U4) | A Venezuelan telenovela was adapted in several countries | Media can move from outside the Western core. |
| Chinese microdramas | contra-flow; platform power (U2) | Chinese content reaches overseas platform audiences | Non-US flows can grow while platform inequality remains. |

**Source:** Mirrlees (2013), Chapter 1; Week 3 tutorial.
