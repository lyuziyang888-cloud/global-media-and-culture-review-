# Unit 3: Cultural Globalisation Evidence

## Best Evidence

### Lucas versus Legrain

**Use for concepts:** media imperialism (U2), cultural globalisation (U3), cultural homogenisation (U1), cultural hybridity (U11)

George Lucas interprets US entertainment exports as Americanisation; Philippe Legrain interprets global circulation as exchange, freedom and cultural hybridity.

**What it proves:** The same media flow can be seen as domination or cultural exchange.

### Jane the Virgin

**Use for concepts:** contra-flow (U3), cultural globalisation (U3), adaptation (U4), format programs (U4), cultural hybridity (U11)

The Venezuelan telenovela *Juana la Virgen* was adapted in the US, South Korea, India and several other markets.

**What it proves:** Media can originate outside the US and move in several directions.

### Chinese Microdramas

**Use for concepts:** contra-flow (U3), cultural globalisation (U3), platform power (U2), platform imperialism (U2)

Chinese microdramas reach overseas audiences through streaming platforms.

**What it proves:** Non-US content can circulate globally, but the flow may remain unequal.

## Exam-Ready Judgement

Cultural globalisation best explains movement, adaptation and audience agency, while media imperialism remains necessary for analysing unequal industrial power.

**Source:** Mirrlees (2013), Chapter 1; Week 3 lecture and tutorial.
