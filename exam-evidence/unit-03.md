# Unit 3: Cultural Globalisation Evidence

## Lucas versus Legrain

**Use for:** Media imperialism (U2); cultural globalisation (U3)

**Evidence:** Lucas views global media as Americanisation, while Legrain emphasises exchange and hybridity.

**What it proves:** The same media flow can be understood as either domination or cultural exchange.

## *Jane the Virgin*

**Use for:** Contra-flow (U3); adaptation (U4)

**Evidence:** A Venezuelan telenovela was adapted for audiences in several countries.

**What it proves:** Media formats can move from outside the Western core into wider markets.

## Chinese Microdramas

**Use for:** Contra-flow; platform power (U2)

**Evidence:** Chinese microdramas reach overseas audiences through digital platforms.

**What it proves:** Non-US media can travel globally, although platforms still influence its visibility.

**Source:** Mirrlees (2013), Chapter 1; Week 3 tutorial.
