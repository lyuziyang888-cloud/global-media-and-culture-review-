# Unit 3: Cultural Globalisation Evidence

## Question: How do contra-flows challenge media imperialism?

**Concepts:** contra-flow; media imperialism

**Evidence:** Chinese microdramas circulate internationally, and Venezuela's *Juana la Virgen* was adapted in the US and other markets.

**Direct answer:** These examples show media moving from outside the traditional Western core into wider markets. This challenges a simple one-way US-to-rest model, although platform and ownership inequality may remain.

## Question: How can one media flow support two paradigms?

**Concepts:** media imperialism; cultural globalisation

**Evidence:** Lucas interprets US exports as Americanisation, while Legrain interprets global circulation as exchange and hybridity.

**Direct answer:** Media imperialism focuses on unequal cultural power. Cultural globalisation focuses on multidirectional circulation, adaptation and audience agency. The better explanation depends on whether the question emphasises control or cultural exchange.
