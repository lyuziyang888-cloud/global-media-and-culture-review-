# Unit 12: Nollywood Evidence

## Official Question: How does Nollywood offer an alternative understanding of digital distribution? Include marketers.

**Concepts:** alternative digital distribution; informality; marketers

**Evidence:** Films circulate through physical markets, Bluetooth, FlashShare and offline downloads. Marketers finance films and control physical distribution.

**Direct answer:** Nollywood shows that digital distribution does not require formal streaming. Informal systems improve access under local infrastructure conditions, while marketer control can reduce transparency and producers' rights.

## Question: Why did iROKOtv localise its service?

**Concepts:** streaming localisation; infrastructure

**Evidence:** iROKOtv tried kiosks, cash or airtime payments, compressed files and low-data transfers.

**Direct answer:** High data costs, payment barriers and unreliable electricity made standard streaming unsuitable. iROKOtv adapted its technology and payment model to local conditions.

## Question: Does formal distribution solve every problem?

**Concepts:** formalisation; corporate power

**Evidence:** Africa Magic and Canal+ expanded Nollywood's reach through formal rights and partnerships.

**Direct answer:** Formal distribution can improve reach and investment, but it may transfer control toward large regional or foreign companies.
