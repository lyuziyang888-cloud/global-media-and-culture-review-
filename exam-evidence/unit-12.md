# Unit 12: Nollywood Evidence

| Case | Use for | Key evidence | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketers | informality; distribution power (U2) | Finance films and control physical distribution | Distribution control creates industry power. |
| Bluetooth and market stalls | alternative digital distribution; piracy | Low-data transfers work with limited infrastructure | Digital distribution does not require streaming. |
| iROKOtv | streaming localisation (U5); media capacity (U6) | Kiosks, compressed files and local payments | Services can adapt to local conditions. |
| Netflix | platform imperialism (U2); infrastructure | Credit cards and data-heavy streaming fit poorly | A global model may not suit every market. |
| Canal+ and Africa Magic | formalisation; corporate power | Formal rights expand regional circulation | Reach can grow while control shifts to large firms. |

**Source:** Miller (2021), pp. 259-276.
