# Unit 2: Media Imperialism Evidence

## Reading Claim

Jin argues that Facebook renews media imperialism through US-centred ownership, data extraction, technological dominance and asymmetrical global capital flows.

## Best Examples

### Facebook Data

**Concepts:** platform capitalism, data commodification

Users communicate voluntarily while Facebook converts their attention and data into targeted-advertising revenue.

**Use:** Facebook shows how apparently free participation produces commercial value for the corporation that owns the platform.

### Global Platform Dominance

**Concepts:** platform imperialism, network effects

Facebook was the leading social network in 152 of 167 analysed countries in January 2018.

**Use:** Facebook illustrates platform imperialism because users are globally dispersed while technological control and capital accumulation remain concentrated in a US corporation.

### Hollywood and the Pentagon

**Concepts:** ideology, state power, media imperialism

Cooperation between entertainment producers and the US military is described as militainment.

**Use:** Militainment demonstrates that media power can reinforce geopolitical interests as well as generate commercial profit.

**Source:** Jin (2019), pp. 187-198; Week 2 lecture.
