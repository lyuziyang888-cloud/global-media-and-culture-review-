# Unit 2: Media Imperialism Evidence

| Case | Use for | Key evidence | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook data | platform capitalism; platform imperialism | User data and attention become advertising revenue | User activity creates profit for the platform owner. |
| Facebook's global reach | media imperialism; platform imperialism | Led social networking in 152 of 167 countries in 2018 | Global users can remain under US corporate control. |
| Hollywood and Pentagon | media imperialism; ideology | Military-entertainment cooperation is called “militainment” | Media can support state power and profit. |

**Source:** Jin (2019), pp. 187-198.
