# Unit 2: Media Imperialism Evidence

## Facebook Data

**Use for:** Platform capitalism; platform imperialism

**Evidence:** Facebook collects users' data and attention and turns them into advertising revenue.

**What it proves:** Users create economic value for the company that owns the platform.

## Facebook's Global Reach

**Use for:** Media imperialism; platform imperialism

**Evidence:** In 2018, Facebook led social networking in 152 of 167 countries.

**What it proves:** A platform can have global users while remaining under US corporate control.

## Hollywood and the Pentagon

**Use for:** Media imperialism; ideology

**Evidence:** Hollywood and the US military cooperate in a relationship described as “militainment.”

**What it proves:** Media can promote state power while also generating commercial profit.

**Source:** Jin (2019), pp. 187-198.
