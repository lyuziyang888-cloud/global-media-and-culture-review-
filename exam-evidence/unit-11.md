## Evidence Details

### Cover Dance as Participation

**Use for concepts:** participatory globalisation, cultural hybridity, fan agency

Yoon describes cover dances, reaction videos, parodies and fan forums as ways fans rework K-pop (pp. 379-380).

**What it proves:** Cover dances are a form of active fan participation.

### Reinterpretation from Local Experience

**Use for concepts:** cosmopolitan globalisation, cultural flow, localisation

Cover dancers reinterpret K-pop through their own experiences and locations (pp. 380-382).

**What it proves:** Fans give K-pop new meanings in local settings.

### Community Beyond Nationality

**Use for concepts:** transcultural fandom, affective affinity, identity

Jin describes transcultural fandom as a space where people from different genders, cultures and ethnicities form connections around shared interests.

**What it proves:** Shared interests can connect fans across national borders.

### Limits of Participation

**Use for concepts:** fan labour, platform power, corporate power

Fan participation can create value for advertisers and platform providers (Yoon, p. 380).

**What it proves:** Fans have agency, but platforms and companies still hold power.

### KATSEYE's Hybrid Industrial Strategy

**Use for concepts:** cultural hybridity, globalisation, localisation, corporate power

HYBE partnered with Geffen Records to create KATSEYE for a US debut.

**What it proves:** K-pop production can be combined with global identities and languages.

## Exam-Ready Judgement

K-pop fandom is participatory and transcultural when fans actively adapt media and form connections across borders. The judgement should remain tied to the chosen evidence because not every act of viewing or imitation proves hybridity.

**Sources:** Yoon (2018), pp. 373-389; Jin (2024), pp. 118-134; [Korea JoongAng Daily (2024), "K-pop's most diverse newcomers KATSEYE aim to push boundaries"](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/k-pops-most-diverse-newcomers-katseye-aim-to-push-boundaries-in-the-music-scene/12117156).
