# Unit 6: Media Capacity Evidence

| Case | Use for | Key evidence | Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indian animation outsourcing | world factory; media imperialism (U2) | Builds skills while foreign firms may retain IP | Outsourcing can build skills without full local control. |
| *Hari Puttar* | mimetic isomorphism | Copies a proven model | Imitation may succeed but weaken originality and IP. |
| Co-productions and remakes | technology transfer; formats (U4) | Partners share production knowledge | Collaboration can transfer skills. |
| *Hero* | niche breakthrough; contra-flow (U3) | Distinctive content reaches overseas markets | Local content can gain global reach. |
| Seoul Digital Media City | cultural milieu; media capitals (U3) | Firms, workers and infrastructure cluster together | Connected local resources support repeated production. |

**Source:** Keane (2006), pp. 839-847, 852-853.
