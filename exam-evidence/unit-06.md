# Unit 6: Media Capacity Evidence

## Indian Animation Outsourcing

**Use for:** World factory; media imperialism (U2)

**Evidence:** Indian workers gain production skills, but foreign companies may retain the intellectual property.

**What it proves:** Outsourcing can build local skills without giving local producers full control.

## *Hari Puttar*

**Use for:** Mimetic isomorphism

**Evidence:** The film imitates an already successful cultural model.

**What it proves:** Copying a proven model may reduce originality and local ownership of ideas.

## Co-Productions and Remakes

**Use for:** Technology transfer; formats (U4)

**Evidence:** Production partners share knowledge while working on the same project.

**What it proves:** International collaboration can transfer skills to local producers.

## *Hero*

**Use for:** Niche breakthrough; contra-flow (U3)

**Evidence:** A culturally distinctive Chinese film reached overseas markets.

**What it proves:** Local content can achieve international success by keeping its distinctive features.

## Seoul Digital Media City

**Use for:** Cultural milieu; media capitals (U3)

**Evidence:** Media firms, workers and infrastructure are concentrated in the same area.

**What it proves:** A connected local cluster can support regular production and knowledge sharing.

**Source:** Keane (2006), pp. 839-847, 852-853.
