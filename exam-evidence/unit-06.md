# Unit 6: Media Capacity Evidence

## Question: Which Keane model best builds lasting media capacity?

**Concepts:** media capacity; one selected Keane model

**Evidence options:**
- **World factory:** Indian animation outsourcing builds skills, but foreign firms may retain IP.
- **Mimetic isomorphism:** *Hari Puttar* and copied dramas may succeed but weaken originality.
- **Cultural technology transfer:** co-productions and remakes transfer production knowledge.
- **Niche breakthrough:** *Hero* and *House of Flying Daggers* take distinctive content abroad.
- **Cultural/industrial milieu:** Seoul Digital Media City connects firms, workers and infrastructure.

**Direct answer:** Cultural/industrial milieu provides the strongest long-term capacity because local firms, workers and knowledge remain connected. One successful export is weaker evidence than an industry that can produce repeatedly.

## Question: Does international success prove media capacity?

**Concepts:** niche breakthrough; media capacity

**Evidence:** A film may reach foreign audiences without giving local producers control over IP, skills or infrastructure.

**Direct answer:** No. International visibility is useful, but lasting capacity requires local control and repeated production.
