## Central Question

The task is not simply asking whether the company distributes content internationally. It asks whether the company has developed the capacity to participate in the global media economy with meaningful control over production, creativity, intellectual property, partnerships and market access.

Your final judgement should answer both questions:

```text
Is the company still peripheral?
To what extent does it globalise on its own terms?
```

Avoid treating "global" and "peripheral" as simple opposites. A company may achieve global distribution while remaining dependent on foreign platforms, finance, technology or intellectual property.

## Analysis Pattern

Use the following movement in each body paragraph:

```text
Keane category
-> exact meaning
-> specific company activity
-> explanation of what the evidence demonstrates
-> limitation or dependency
-> connection to global capacity
```

Description reports what the company does. Critical analysis explains what that activity demonstrates about capacity, who controls it, what makes it possible and what limits it.

## High-Value Distinctions

### Global Reach vs Global Capacity

International availability is evidence of reach. Capacity concerns the company's ability to create, finance, own, adapt and circulate content.

### Partnership vs Dependency

An international partnership can expand capacity, but it may also reveal dependence if another company controls finance, intellectual property, technology or distribution.

### Corporate Claims vs Demonstrated Activity

The official website provides useful primary evidence, but its claims must be examined rather than repeated.

### Visibility vs Operating on Its Own Terms

A company may be globally visible without controlling the conditions of its participation. Examine bargaining power, ownership and strategic decision-making.

## Possible Judgement Models

Use one of these logical positions only if the evidence supports it:

```text
The company is no longer meaningfully peripheral because it controls
several capacities and uses partnerships strategically.
```

```text
The company has developed substantial global reach but remains partly
peripheral because key capacities depend on external corporations.
```

```text
The core-periphery label is too rigid: the company exercises autonomy
in some categories while remaining dependent in others.
```
