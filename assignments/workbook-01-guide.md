# Workbook 1 Guide

## Task Requirements From Tutorial 2

Choose one supplied Insight Report about a recent global-media trend or change. Submit a response of no more than 500 words, excluding the title and references, before attending the tutorial.

The response must:

1. briefly explain the report topic
2. analyse how a media-imperialism scholar would interpret it
3. analyse how a cultural-globalisation scholar would interpret it
4. use at least two key ideas from each paradigm
5. use Chapter 1 of the Mirrlees reading
6. use no outside readings
7. argue which paradigm better explains the report

## Assessment Criteria

- effective understanding and application of both paradigms
- critical analysis of the report and evidence of understanding its data
- a persuasive argument supporting the selected paradigm

## Recommended 500-Word Structure

| Section | Approximate words | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Introduction | 50-60 | identify the trend and state which paradigm explains it better |
| Media imperialism | 140-160 | apply two key ideas to specific report data |
| Cultural globalisation | 140-160 | apply two key ideas to the same trend and data |
| Comparative judgement | 100-120 | weigh explanatory strengths and limitations |
| Conclusion | 30-40 | answer the question directly |

## High-Value Distinction

Description tells the reader what the report says. Analysis explains why the pattern exists, what a paradigm makes visible, what it overlooks and why one interpretation is more convincing.

## Do Not Draft Yet If Missing

- the selected Insight Report
- the complete assigned Mirrlees Chapter 1
- the formal Canvas rubric, if it contains more detail than the tutorial slide

Without those materials, the repository can prepare the framework but cannot safely supply report evidence, cultural-globalisation ideas or final citations.
